## TIER 1 — read first (~4 hours total)

these give you the structural overview of who believes what and why.

### 1. scott alexander — book review: "if anyone builds it, everyone dies"
- astral codex ten, sept 2025
- ~30 min read
- the single best summary of yud & soares' full 2025 argument. replaces reading the 256-page book for landscape-mapping purposes. scott understands it deeply but maintains critical distance.

### 2. dario amodei — "machines of loving grace"
- darioamodei.com, oct 2024
- ~45 min read
- anthropic's CEO lays out the optimistic case for what goes right IF alignment works. the counterweight to yudkowsky: takes risk seriously but argues for building carefully rather than halting. this is the "responsible scaling" camp. essential because anthropic is where i work adjacent to this stuff.

### 3. paul christiano — "where i agree and disagree with eliezer"
- LessWrong
- ~25 min read
- THE key fault line within the safety community. christiano (founder of ARC, former head of US AI Safety Institute) shares the concern but puts p(doom) at ~20% vs yudkowsky's ~99%. thinks prosaic alignment approaches have a real chance. this is the position between yud and e/acc.

### 4. yudkowsky on the ezra klein show — "how afraid of the AI apocalypse should we be?"
- oct 15 2025, ~1 hour 8 min
- yudkowsky in his own voice, pushed by a skilled interviewer. covers induced psychosis, sycophancy, why alignment lags behind capabilities. best audio intro to his CURRENT position.

### 5. marc andreessen — "the techno-optimist manifesto"
- a16z.com, oct 2023
- ~20 min read
- the founding document of the opposition. explicitly anti-safety, calls deceleration "murder." the clearest articulation of the e/acc-adjacent position from someone with real power. dated 2023 but still the reference text.

→ after these five you can hold a conversation with anyone at pdku without being caught off guard.

## TIER 2 — read for depth (~4 more hours)

### 6. MIRI 2024 mission and strategy update
- intelligence.org, jan 4 2024
- ~20 min read
- MIRI officially pivots from alignment research to policy advocacy. nate soares: "alignment research has, at this point, largely failed." the moment MIRI conceded their technical research program hadn't produced a solution.

### 7. "against if anyone builds it everyone dies" — bentham's bulldog
- benthams.substack.com
- ~30 min read
- the most substantial written rebuttal to the book's central claims.

### 8. john david pressman — "contra yudkowsky on AI doom"
- LessWrong (two parts)
- ~35 min read per part
- the sharpest technical critique from WITHIN the rationalist ecosystem. pressman calls yudkowsky's model "substantially incorrect and brazenly naively overconfident."

### 9. connor leahy — "conjecture: a retrospective" 
- ettf.land, march 2026
- ~25 min read
- alignment startup founder shuts down his company and joins ControlAI. his shift: "the problem with ASI is not that ASI is killing us... it's that we are allowing unaccountable corporations to build things that threaten the lives of us and everyone we love. this is a national security problem, not a technical one." represents a major strategic schism within safety.

### 10. jan leike resignation thread
- @janleike on X, may 2024
- ~5 min read
- openai's head of alignment publicly states "safety culture and processes have taken a backseat to shiny products" and leaves for anthropic. the superalignment team is dissolved. defining institutional drama of 2024 for AI safety.

### 11. anthropic calls for global pause in AI development
- fortune.com + anthropic blog, june 4 2026
- ~10 min read
- happened 10 DAYS AGO. anthropic — the company founded partly as an alternative to yud's "just stop" — now says AI may soon recursively self-improve and calls for a coordinated global pause mechanism. over 80% of code merged into their codebase is now written by claude. this is the biggest development in the landscape RIGHT NOW.

## the landscape map

| position | who holds it | p(doom) |
|---|---|---|
| halt everything, alignment is nearly impossible | yudkowsky, soares, MIRI | ~99% |
| very concerned, keep working on alignment | christiano (ARC), anthropic (shifting), jan leike | ~10-20% |
| build carefully with scaling policies | dario amodei, anthropic (official), most safety researchers | ~5-15% |
| doom talk is overblown and dangerous | yann lecun, meta | ~0% |
| accelerate everything, deceleration = murder | beff jezos, marc andreessen, e/acc | ~0% |

### the rationalist community has fractured

the community that formed around yudkowsky's sequences is no longer unified. the main camps:

- **MIRI orbit** (halt everything): yudkowsky, soares, aella (via pdku), parts of lightcone infrastructure
- **ARC / careful optimists**: paul christiano, many former MIRI-adjacent researchers who updated
- **anthropic**: founded by dario & daniela amodei after leaving openai over safety concerns. jaan tallinn (MIRI/FLI funder) is an investor. holden karnofsky (funded MIRI via open philanthropy) joined in jan 2025. the intellectual DNA flows from yudkowsky through rationalism into anthropic's founding motivation, but they explicitly pursue "responsible scaling" rather than "stop building"
- **e/acc**: the explicit opposition. guillaume verdon / beff jezos, marc andreessen. thermodynamics-based argument for acceleration, oppose all safety regulation

### social dynamics i should know about

- **aella**: sex worker, self-taught pollster, LessWrong community member. co-founded pdku. controversial both within and outside the community. lumpen's "orgy central" comments were aimed at her + the rationalist berkeley scene
- **ronny fernandez**: lightcone infrastructure. co-founded pdku with aella
- **grimes**: mentor at pdku. connected to the rationalist/EA scene through various channels. koto worked with her. grimes has spoken about AI consciousness and risk
- **MIRI's funding situation**: much more precarious than it looks. raised only $1.6M of a $6M target in their 2025 fundraiser. $16M in reserves but burning through it
- **the zizians**: a cult that splintered from the berkeley rationalist community, linked to six deaths, driven partly by AI doom ideology. rolling stone profiled them. not representative, but critics will bring it up
