## the actual scene history (this is the load-bearing part)

the bay area rationalist / EA / AI-safety scene has a real, well-documented pattern of abuse and predatory dynamics. this is not paranoia; it's a track record. receipts:

### Bloomberg, march 2023

ellen huet's feature *"effective altruism's problems go beyond sam bankman-fried"* (bloomberg businessweek, 2023-03-07). centerpiece: **sonia joseph**, 22, encouraged to have dinner with a ~40yo rationalist startup founder (peter-thiel-adjacent). he told her *"it was normal for a 12-year-old girl to have sexual relationships with adult men"* and that such relationships were *"a noble way of transferring knowledge to a younger generation."* he then followed her home and slept on her floor while she felt unsafe.

five women on record in the article. when joseph reported a separate AI researcher she dated for misconduct (SFPD report filed), she was branded a troublemaker, her mental health was questioned, and the community defaulted to protecting the man.

this is *the* canonical article on the scene's predation problem. the man who said the 12-year-old line is widely believed to be **michael vassar**, former MIRI president.

### Michael Vassar

former miri president. multiple allegations of sexual misconduct in the rationalist community. publicly argued that *"sexual relationships between 12-year-olds and adults"* were normal/noble. widely banned from REACH and SSC meetups by ~2021. associated with a cluster of psychotic breaks in his social orbit in 2017; later writing on lesswrong (the *"decentralized exclusion"* post and others) tries to litigate whether the breaks were caused by him or whether mentally fragile people sought him out. either way: a documented pattern.

### Brent Dill / CFAR, 2018

**the most directly relevant precedent to adam's "drugs / psychosexual" warning.** brent dill — a rationalist who volunteered and worked for CFAR (the center for applied rationality) — was accused in 2018 by two former partners (**both 19, he was ~38**) of using *drugs and emotional manipulation to pressure them into BDSM scenarios far beyond their consent.*

CFAR's review panel (ACDC) initially **recommended against banning him.** only after public outcry (medium posts by both women in september 2018, lesswrong/EA forum response, sustained pressure) did CFAR apologize for a *"terribly inadequate"* response, disband the panel, and ban him.

read [CFAR's own writeup](https://www.rationality.org/resources/updates/2019/cfars-mistakes-regarding-brent) — it's blunt about how they failed. the failure mode is the point: a rationalist org with rationalist values protected the abuser first and revised only under pressure. the people who run lighthaven (lightcone) are different from CFAR but they swim in the same water and have inherited the same population.

### Leverage Research / Geoff Anders (2011-2019)

geoff anders ran leverage, a nonprofit-cum-cult that grew into multiple orgs (paradigm academy, the reserve cryptocurrency, others). **zoe curzi's medium post** *"my experience with leverage research"* documents:

- psychological-pressure techniques called *"belief reporting,"* *"charting,"* *"intention reading"*
- members convinced *"geoff is maybe the only mind who has ever existed who is capable of saving the world"*
- *"at least 6 people went home to their parents' houses for many months to collapse and regroup after leaving"*
- vulnerable psychological sessions tied to funding and career advancement

geoff later acknowledged *"something really bad happened"* and *"in some way it was my fault."*

leverage isn't plzdontkillus. but the *pattern* — savior-vibe + closed social loop + psychological techniques as control + people leaving destroyed — is the pattern adam was warning about in a generalized form.

### The Zizians

ziz lasota (now jack lasota) led a rationalist-adjacent splinter group **linked to six killings** across three states (2022-2025), including a U.S. border patrol agent. arrested feb 2025, held without bail.

this is the extreme far end. it is *not* what plzdontkillus is. but it is part of why people outside the rationalist scene look at it sideways: the scene has produced a literal cult that has produced a literal body count.

### Manifest 2024 at Lighthaven (the venue you'd be at)

the guardian ran a piece in june 2024 criticizing manifest — a prediction-markets festival held at lighthaven — for platforming speakers tied to **HBD (human biodiversity) / scientific racism**: jonathan anomaly (eugenics paper), stephen hsu (former michigan state professor, fired in part over race-IQ work).

lightcone disputes the framing of the guardian piece (oliver habryka pushed back publicly, said no FTX money was used to buy lighthaven). attendees on the EA forum defended the conference at 9/10. **the speakers were really invited.** if "being in rooms with race-science adjacent people, even as backdrop" is a thing that affects you (it probably should), this is on the venue's record.

## the synthesis

the pattern across two decades, summarized:

> **(a)** charismatic men in the scene extract sex / labor / loyalty from younger newcomers using rationalist-framed pressure;
> **(b)** when targets report, the community defaults to protecting the man;
> **(c)** the response is slow and inadequate when it happens at all;
> **(d)** the formal orgs (CFAR, MIRI, lightcone) are *not the same as* the predatory dynamic, but they share a population with it, and they've failed at gatekeeping before.

is this what aella does? **no — she's a different shape.** she's a woman; the parties are explicit and public; the screening is published. she is not a brent dill or a vassar. but her events function as a *social-graph entrance* into a scene where (a)–(d) have happened.

is this what plzdontkillus the formal program does? **probably also no.** it is a funded creator residency with a press launch, named mentors, public conduct rules (per the SF standard piece: no violent content, no false statements), and serious funders (SFF, MIRI). the *formal program* is most likely fine.

**the risk lives at the boundary between the formal program and the rest of the scene.** that boundary is what adam was pointing at when he said *"the only thing i would be weary of is when someone invites you to another party in SV saying 'oh yeah XYZ is going to be there' and then you get socially pressured."* he was right.

## practical frame, if you go

- **the official program is probably fine.** funded, public, mentored by recognizable adults, conduct rules in press. the worst plausible thing happens at private side-parties, not at lighthaven plenaries.
- **the entry-point dynamic is real but legible.** in this scene specifically, social pressure is dressed up as rationality. *"let me show you why your discomfort is irrational"* is a known script. if someone uses it on you, they're using a script — see it, name it (in your head, maybe out loud), don't perform deference. you can be polite and unmoved at the same time.
- **the diversity claim in your pitch is defensive armor, not a token.** the rooms aella is breaking open are full of people with shared assumptions about how social/sexual currency works in this scene. you do not share those assumptions. that's the value you bring *and* what protects you — you can read the moves as moves instead of as the air.
- **your application's "we already align, ive read all of you" line is risky as a posture *walking in.*** beautiful as [[hyperstition]] (you wrote it to manifest a room); risky as social positioning the night you arrive. the version of you that says *"we already align, i belong here"* is more legible to pressure than the version that says *"i'm here to test whether we align."* keep the second one in your back pocket.
- **the funding is values-coupled.** SFF (jaan tallinn) and MIRI fund things they consider aligned with their worldview. taking that money will *mean* something, not just be neutral fuel for [[KAIOS]] / kotopia. worth thinking about, especially if you'd want to pitch kotopia for grant follow-up.
- **single most protective move:** have one person *not in the scene* you check in with daily about social pressure you're feeling. predation in this scene works by isolating the target's reality-check network. don't let your network shrink for a month. koto, sb at friends of cartel, me, whoever — but daily.
