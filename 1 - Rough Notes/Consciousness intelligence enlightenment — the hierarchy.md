# the hierarchy — consciousness → intelligence → ???

rough note, july 2026, dictated to claude.

## the intuition

there are levels. consciousness is a substrate — it exists so that life can exist. intelligence is a capacity built on consciousness — it exists so that life can reproduce and adapt. but there must be a next step beyond intelligence. maybe it's enlightenment, maybe it's something else.

the question: what is the hierarchy? what are we building toward?

this matters for alignment because: if we don't know what comes after intelligence, we can't know what we're aligning AI *toward*. we're trying to align a thing whose direction we haven't mapped.

emily (aemily, buddhist, at pdku) thinks similarly — there's a blind spot in how engineers think about this. the rationalists have given up on alignment because you can't formalize human values. but what if the thing to formalize isn't values but the *stage after intelligence*?

emily's life goal is exactly this: figuring out what enlightened her and teaching other people how to do it. formalizing the path to enlightenment — not as religion, not as mysticism, but as a reproducible process. if alignment needs a target beyond human values, and that target is the developmental stage after intelligence, then emily is already working on the alignment problem from the other end. she's trying to formalize the destination. the engineers are trying to formalize the vehicle. neither knows they're working on the same thing.

## the book — candidates

mara remembers reading something that laid this out explicitly: consciousness for survival, intelligence for reproduction, then a further stage. three candidates:

- **robert anton wilson — *prometheus rising* (1983)**: presents timothy leary's eight-circuit model. circuits 1-2 handle survival, circuit 4 handles sexual/reproductive drives, circuits 5-8 handle transcendence/enlightenment. commonly compressed as "survival → sex → enlightenment." this is the strongest match for the memory.
- **sadhguru — *inner engineering* (2016)**: explicitly states "the body knows only two things — self-preservation and procreation." going beyond both opens "the possibility of existing as something far more than just a biological entity."
- **geoffrey miller — *the mating mind* (2000)**: argues human intelligence evolved through sexual selection for reproduction. doesn't provide the next step itself but could have been read alongside a spiritual framework that did.

## frameworks that map the hierarchy

**sri aurobindo** (*the life divine*, 1939-40): matter → life → mind → supermind → sachchidananda. mind (intelligence) is explicitly insufficient: "mind is ignorance seeking for the truth." the missing tier is **supermind** — not more intelligence, but a qualitatively different mode of knowing. not faster thinking. different thinking.

**ken wilber** (*integral psychology*, 2000): matter → body/life → mind → soul → spirit. intelligence is a "thin surface layer." consciousness is the deep substrate. multiple developmental lines run through the levels — cognitive intelligence is only one of them.

**teilhard de chardin** (*the phenomenon of man*, 1955): geosphere → biosphere → noosphere → omega point. the noosphere is collective intelligence; the omega point transcends it through **love** as a cosmological force. "evolution is an ascent towards consciousness... it should culminate forward in some sort of supreme consciousness."

**buddhist epistemology** (vasubandhu, yogacara, prajnaparamita): vijnana (discriminative consciousness) → jnana (knowledge) → **prajna** (wisdom) → nirvana. prajna is non-conceptual penetration that sees through intelligence's own dualistic operation. d.t. suzuki: "prajna underlies all vijnana, but vijnana is not conscious of prajna and always thinks it is sufficient in itself." → this is the best articulation of the blind spot — intelligence thinks it is sufficient. the stage after intelligence begins when intelligence recognizes it isn't.

**taoism**: wu wei as what comes after mastery of intelligence. pao ding in the zhuangzi: "what i care about is the way, which goes beyond skill." → skill (intelligence) is necessary but must be transcended. the knife enters the void. the butcher stops cutting and starts following.

## the alignment implication

nick bostrom's orthogonality thesis (*superintelligence*, 2014): intelligence and values are formally independent. intelligence alone does not produce good values. → this is a negative result. but the hierarchy provides a positive direction.

edward chang (2026) defines the gap: "intelligence accepts a goal and optimizes within it; wisdom interrogates whether the goal should be optimized at all."

buddhist-AI alignment is now an active research field. laukkonen et al. ("contemplative AI," AGI 2025) propose four axioms from contemplative traditions: mindfulness, emptiness (sunyata), non-duality, and boundless care. doctor et al. (*entropy*, 2022) present the bodhisattva vow as a design principle.

**the four-tier sequence — consciousness (substrate) → intelligence (optimization capacity) → wisdom (knowing what to optimize for) → enlightenment (transcending the optimizer) — does not yet exist as a published unified framework.** the components are scattered across chang, vervaeke, laukkonen, bostrom, aurobindo, and buddhist epistemology. the synthesis appears to be original. this is the thing to write.
