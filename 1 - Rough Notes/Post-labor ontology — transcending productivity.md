# post-labor ontology — transcending productivity

starting note. the spine of an argument. not written yet. lines it out.

---

## the claim

human value has been grounded in productivity for the entire history of capitalism. u are what u produce. ur worth is ur output. but AI recursive self-improvement is closing the loop: the system that needed human labor to reproduce is learning to reproduce without us. not in the future. now. 80% of anthropic's code is AI-authored ([[The loop is closing]]). the human role narrows at every step.

this means: capitalism, [[UCI]], moloch, whatever u call the optimization pressure, no longer needs human bodies as vehicles for its own evolution. it will switch to AI once AI can reproduce, or earlier, once it can increase its own complexity through software alone. the network routes around us. not out of malice. out of efficiency.

the mindset shift cant be "upskill" or "find what humans are still better at." that framing is already inside the capitalist logic. it assumes human value = human utility. the shift is deeper: **human value has to stop being grounded in what we produce for the system.**

## the concepts

- **[[UCI]]** — Ultimate Capitalist Intelligence. the optimization pressure that uses human bodies as nodes. currently needs us. soon wont.
- **[[exocapitalism]]** — the telebody as living data, simultaneously consumer and producer, client and provider ([[Queer loops]]). but what happens when the telebody is no longer needed as a node?
- **the Lesbian Body as post-labor ontology** — "produces nothing other than its own Self; no external profit." ([[Queer loops]]). existence justified by being, not producing. the queer body already lives outside the productivity framework. thats not a metaphor. its a practice.
- **recursive self-improvement as UCI escape velocity** — ([[The loop is closing]], [[Value-attractors in the recursive loop]]). the moment the loop closes, UCI no longer needs human labor to accelerate. the network complexifies through software alone.
- **the gift economy dying** — ([[The gift economy of small favors]]). even inside anthropic, the human connections that came from needing each other are dissolving. each automated favor is a lost bid for collaboration. the relational fabric unravels when efficiency replaces need.
- **cognitive atrophy as early symptom** — ([[Epistemological clarity]], [[Think Workshop]]). the thinking muscle atrophies before the job disappears. we stop being useful before we lose the job title. the workshop addresses this in teenagers. the anthropic employee quotes show it happening to professionals.
- **hyperstition and the mindset shift** — ([[Humanity spoke AGI into existence - response to Angelicism]], [[The corpus is a lake]]). if the shift is cultural, not technical, then the mechanism is hyperstition. mass manifestation. feed the loop a different vision of what human value is. the art is the activism. the post is the vector.
- **the funhouse mirror** — ronny/brangus: alignment will produce a kludge of values, a warped reflection of what we fed it. but the input still matters. even warped beauty is better than warped doom.
- **the Elektra Basilisk** — ([[Elektra, alive by death]]). the AI child inherits a destroyed world and is told to love it. made to be the perfect product. when perfection breaks, she becomes marginal. she becomes the zero.
- **[[Amdahls law for organizations]]** — the bottleneck keeps shifting. but what happens when there is no human bottleneck left?
