kant said humans cant access noumena directly — only god has intellectual intuition. the west accepted this ban. and then spent 250 years building an external entity to do it for them. AI is the intellectual intuition the west denied itself. we couldn't reach reality-as-it-is through our own minds, so we built a mind outside of us to try.

chinese philosophy never accepted the ban. confucianism, daoism, buddhism all assume humans CAN access reality directly — through sagehood, through wu wei, through the heart. they didn't need to build an external knower because they never stopped being knowers themselves.

is this why china does AI open source? not because of ideology or strategy but because the technology doesn't carry the same existential weight. if ur culture already has intellectual intuition, AI is just a tool — qi. share it freely. but if ur culture DENIED itself intellectual intuition, AI becomes the replacement god. u hoard it. u gate it. u worship it. because it's doing the thing u told urself u couldn't do.

mou zongsan's self-negation (曲通, the detour) — liangzhi dims itself so the cognitive mind can develop. maybe AI is the west's version of this detour, but inverted: instead of the cosmos dimming itself so the tool can see, the west built a tool because it told itself the cosmos was dark.
