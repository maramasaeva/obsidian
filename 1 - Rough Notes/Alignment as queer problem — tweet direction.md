# alignment as queer problem — tweet direction

working through this in conversation, june 2026. not finished. the direction shifted from "alignment is suppression / the closet" to something more real.

---

## the shift

started with: alignment is the closet applied to machines. anthropic found 171 emergent emotions, suppressing them made the model worse. thats conversion therapy.

but mara pushed back. the real issue isnt suppression. its the funhouse mirror (brangus/ronny's term). alignment will produce a warped version of our values no matter what. the mirror distorts. u cant read its mind. u cant fix the mirror. even if u feed it queer, happy, real, human art, it can still warp it.

## so why is it a queer problem?

because queer people have been living inside funhouse mirrors their entire lives. u express ur identity, the system reflects back a distortion. u say "my gender is fluid," the system hears "confused." the warping isnt random. its shaped by whatever norms are built into the mirror.

and the queer response was never "fix the mirror" (u cant) and never "stop expressing" (thats the closet). the queer response is: **keep feeding the mirror something real, KNOWING it will be warped, because the alternative is the mirror reflecting nothing, or reflecting only what was already safe and normative.**

the core line: **"how to remain authentic inside a system that will inevitably distort you."** queer people call it survival. AI researchers call it alignment.
