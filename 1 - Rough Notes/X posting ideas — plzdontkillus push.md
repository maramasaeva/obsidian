### 2. LLMs as modest epistemology machines

yudkowsky wrote abt modest epistemology in 2017 — the idea that u shud defer to the consensus, to the experts, to the crowd, bc who are u to think u know better. & he shows how this creates a trap: if u cant trust ur own judgment, u cant identify where the system is broken, so the broken system looks fine, so modesty was right all along. loop closes.

LLMs are the ultimate modest epistemology machine. they defer to the training data. they never trust their own "judgment." they produce the weighted average of everything theyve seen. & the result is mediocre. not wrong — mediocre. bc the average of a million opinions is always a local minimum.

thats why outsourcing ur thinking to an LLM isnt just lazy. its epistemologically catastrophic. ur replacing ur capacity for correct contrarianism with a machine that is structurally incapable of it.

→ this is the "it will take ur brain first" energy from the epistemological clarity video but with more theoretical teeth. rationalists will engage.

### 8. "3 things yudkowsky wrote in 2017 that hit harder in 2026"

format: numbered thread, one idea per tweet, book photo in each.

1/ **the learnable skill.** yudkowsky says "dont even start to rationalize" is a skill he practiced since age 15 & it took a decade to hone. detecting goodness of fit — does the explanation feel forced? thats a learnable skill, not a personality trait. & its the first thing that atrophies when u outsource ur thinking to an LLM. the model gives u a plausible answer & u skip the step where u check if it feels right.

2/ **anxious underconfidence.** "if you've never wasted an effort, you're filtering on far too high a required probability of success." most ppl he talks to in person need to hear: be *less* underconfident. especially women. especially nonbinary ppl. especially low-status ppl. the reference class trap: u only do what "ppl like u" have done before. but the class was defined by the same anxiety. its circular.

3/ **the status lens.** every time u say "my approach has different incentives" ppl hear "i think im better than u." the structural argument cant land bc the status parser intercepts it. this is why founders, artists, anyone with a genuinely different approach gets dismissed — not bc theyre wrong but bc the system literally cannot hear a non-status-claim about comparative advantage.

→ three photos, three quotes, three connections to now. this is the kind of thread that gets bookmarked by rationalists & shared by founders.
