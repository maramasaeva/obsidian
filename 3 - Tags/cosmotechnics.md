# cosmotechnics

the thesis that there is no universal "technology" — only technics embedded in the cosmological conditions of the culture that produced them. coined by yuk hui in *the question concerning technology in china* (2016).

the opposite of the assumption that technics is anthropologically universal. if cosmotechnics is true, then every claim about "technology" that doesn't specify *whose* cosmology it emerges from is either incomplete or colonial.
