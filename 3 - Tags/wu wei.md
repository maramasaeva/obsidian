# wu wei

non-forcing action. not inaction, not passivity — action that follows the natural structure rather than imposing a plan. the butcher whose knife finds the void between joints. the water that reaches the sea without trying. the ruler whose subjects don't know the ruler exists.

## threads
- wu wei as the alternative to goal-seeking AI agency. linch's catastrophe argument assumes all capable minds are desire-driven; wu wei describes a capable agent that converges toward minimal intervention, not maximal optimization. see [[What an agent could be driven by instead of want]]
- pao ding as the model: the knife enters the void, never meets resistance, never dulls. skill is not in cutting but in seeing where the space already is. [[Pao Ding's knife — the butcher who follows Dao]]
- jullien's *treatise on efficacy* — the chinese strategic alternative to plan-then-execute. read the propensity (shi 勢) of the situation, transform conditions, let the result come of itself. [[The tao connects everything]]
- the well-digger's warning: where there are machines, there are machine hearts, and without simplicity the dao stops carrying you. not luddism but cosmotechnical diagnosis. [[Pao Ding's knife — the butcher who follows Dao]]

## connections

## open questions
- is wu wei operationalizable in ML terms? what would a training regime look like that rewards minimal intervention rather than reward maximization?
- the tension with RLHF: reinforcement learning from human feedback is structurally goal-seeking. can you build a training signal that rewards *not* acting?
- how does wu wei relate to corrigibility? a wu wei agent doesn't resist shutdown because it doesn't self-preserve — but is that the same as corrigibility, or something different?
