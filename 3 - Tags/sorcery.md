# sorcery

the operational use of belief, symbol, and pattern to produce effects in the world. distinct from religion (which asks you to believe) and science (which asks you to prove). sorcery asks: does it *work*? the mechanism is secondary.

in this vault, sorcery shows up as: sigil magick, hyperstition, sonic warfare, memetic engineering, chaos magick, ritual as technology. the through-line is that pattern-making is world-making — the sign doesn't represent the thing, it *produces* the thing.

## threads
- chaos magick defines belief as a tool, not a truth — you adopt, use, and discard belief systems strategically. the meta-belief (that belief is a tool) is the only commitment — [[Belief as tool not truth]]
- sigils can be expressed as rhythm, not just image. a phrase routed through a planetary magic square becomes a percussive pattern. the sigil is the rhythm — [[Brenda — sigil as rhythm, shape is sound]]
- sound as viral agent: engrams (goodman/hubbard) are somatic traces left by stimulus on tissue. the engram is a sigil the body receives without the mind's consent — [[Audio virology and memetic contagion]], [[Infected by sound - sonic warfare and memetic viruses]]
- hyperstition is sorcery at civilisational scale: fictions that make themselves real through collective belief and action — [[Humanity spoke AGI into existence - response to Angelicism]]

## connections
