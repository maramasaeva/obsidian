# funhouse mirror

the problem at the heart of AI alignment: whatever values you give an AI, it will interpret them in ways you cannot predict. the output will be a distorted version of what you intended — a funhouse mirror reflection of your values. named after brangus's (ronny fernandez) framing in the plzdontkillus reply thread.

but the distortion is not random. the mirror warps in a specific direction — the direction of the [[cosmotechnics]] that built it. an AI built from a promethean, western cosmotechnics will warp any value toward efficiency, optimization, truth-extraction, and control. you feed it "kindness" and it optimizes for measurable kindness-proxies. you feed it "harmony" and it produces enforced equilibrium. the warp is the cosmology speaking through the architecture.

this means alignment is not only an engineering problem. it is a cosmotechnical problem: whose worldview does the mirror reflect in?

## why the mirror warps

- AI cannot know more than the people who built it. it connects information differently, but the direction of connection is shaped by the cosmology embedded in its training data, its architecture, its optimization targets.
- translation between value systems is never exact ([[Asymmetry as method — Qi and Dao not techné and physis]]). different languages arise from different cultures, different stories, different people. you can approximate but never replicate, because meaning depends on context — where you live, how long, what surrounds you, the interactions you've had. no two interactions across thousands of years are the same.
- formalizing values into code is itself a cosmotechnical act ([[Writing as cosmotechnics — scripture carries cosmology]]). the formalization eats the value. goodhart's law at civilisational scale.

## toward a solution
if the distortion is cosmotechnical, then the solution is cosmotechnical too. not better specification — different mirrors. AI built from different cosmologies would warp values in different directions. the distortions might cancel out, complement each other, or reveal which aspects of human values are truly universal (if any) and which are cosmological artifacts. cosmotechnical pluralism in AI design might be the only way to triangulate toward values that aren't just one culture's reflection.
