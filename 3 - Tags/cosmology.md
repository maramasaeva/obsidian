# cosmology

a culture's deep understanding of how the cosmos works — what the relationship is between humans, nature, the divine, and the order of things. not astronomy, not "the universe" in the physics sense. it's the story a civilization tells itself about what reality is and how everything fits together, and that story determines what kind of technology it builds.

cosmology in this vault is used in yuk hui's sense: the invisible framework that shapes everything downstream. different cosmologies produce different technics, different cities, different justices, different AIs. the greek cosmology produced techné as violence and truth-seeking. the chinese cosmology produced resonance and moral harmony. the question is always: whose cosmology are we building from?
