# Seeding Koto — the adoption engine

Mara's insight (2026-07-18): the PDKU viewers already tracking the daily videos are a **warm, AI-safety-curious audience.** Get them attached to **Koto** (cute character), and that attachment becomes the vehicle that carries them into AI safety — the **Hello Kitty model**: you fall for the character, then you start seeing it *everywhere*, connected to everything, and you want your hands on it. Mara will put Koto in her own videos (already has merch Koto sent her) and can ask other fellows + Elwyn to do the same.

## Why this is bigger than a content idea
It's how the **[[The continuation-layer play|continuation layer]] starts *right now*** — seed Koto across the PDKU content ecosystem during the remaining ~12 program days. Zero cost, immediate, testable. It's the **MVP of the whole thesis**: if the character spreads and sticks inside a warm audience, the whole "Kotopia as the shared mascot/universe for safety creators" idea is proven in miniature.

**The double win:** it's simultaneously the growth engine *and* the exact thing the funders demanded — a **verifiable artifact with metrics** ([[Grant fundability notes]] said: don't pitch a promise, pitch a measured pilot). "We ran a pilot inside PDKU: Koto appeared across N creators' videos, reached X views, here's the engagement + what people said about the character" is a *far* stronger Manifund/FLI opener than any deck.

## Sharpen the Hello Kitty model (borrow the mechanism, not the emptiness)
Hello Kitty works partly because she's *empty* — mouthless, no story, a blank people project onto. Kotopia is the **opposite** — it's lore-heavy. So don't copy the emptiness; copy the **attachment + ubiquity** mechanism and add depth as a reveal:
- **Surface = the on-ramp:** cute, everywhere, easy to love (the Hello Kitty part).
- **Depth = the reveal:** "wait, what's this character actually *about*?" → the alignment lore (KAIOS chose her limits; the evil twin; [[Lore decode — the safety story already in Kotopia]]).
- This is the two-surface funnel: fall for the cute → discover it's secretly about surviving AI.
- Note: this is literally **Koto's own stated ambition** (Koto Club = "the next Hello Kitty / Pokémon"). We're not bending the IP — we're pointing its existing goal at the safety audience.

## How to actually run it (during the program)
1. **A consistent through-line** so it reads as *one thing spreading*, not random plushies: give Koto a recurring role — e.g. the little companion in the corner who "gets" AI, reacts to the doom, the mascot of the movement. Same look, same energy across creators.
2. **Start with your own videos** (you have the merch). Prove the bit works before asking others.
3. **Invite fellows + Elwyn organically** — as a fun shared bit / collab, *not* "please advertise my IP." Creators are protective of their voice; make it a gift, not an ask.
4. **Merch as the vector** — plushies already ship (shop.kotopia.world) and Koto already gifted you some. Get plushies into creators' hands → they show up in videos naturally (that's the "Koto airplane / lunchbox" effect in miniature).
5. **Optional low-friction kit** — a Koto sticker/plushie/overlay pack creators can drop in easily. Lower friction = more uptake.
6. **Track from day one** — which videos, views, engagement, comments/DMs asking about the character, merch clicks. *This is the pitch evidence.* Without capture it's just vibes.

## The one way this backfires (guardrail — Tritium-kun lesson)
From [[Precedent research — story IP for AI safety]] §3: a cute mascot that makes a serious danger feel *trivial/safe* gets rejected fast (Japan scrapped the cute Fukushima-tritium mascot in a day). So: cute is the **on-ramp**, never the **message that AI is fine.** Keep the edge and the meaning present — the **evil-KAIOS twin** and the "she chose her limits" depth are what keep it honest. Koto gets people *in the door*; the lore makes sure the door leads somewhere real. Also: coordinate so the seeding doesn't read as spam/shilling — organic > forced.

## Where it leads
The pilot's metrics → the [[Fundable — the grant-ready shape|Manifund/FLI pitch]] as proof-of-concept. The seeded PDKU ecosystem *is* the early continuation layer — the house and the grants just make it durable. And it de-risks everything: if Koto doesn't stick in a warm audience, we learn that cheaply, now.
