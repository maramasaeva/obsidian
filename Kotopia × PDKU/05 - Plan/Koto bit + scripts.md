# Koto bit + scripts

Assets for the [[Seeding Koto — the adoption engine|seeding pilot]]. The recurring bit, the ask to fellows, and Mara's first video script. *Koto = the cute companion (on-ramp); KAIOS = the AI who chose her limits (the depth people find later). Written around Koto; swap if wanted.*

## The recurring bit — "Koto reacts"

**Character in one line:** Koto is the little companion who feels the AI-doom right along with you — terrified, but rooting for us, and completely unwilling to give up.

**The bit:** Koto is in your video, reacting to the doom. That's it. Three effort tiers so anyone can do it:
- **Cameo (zero effort):** the Koto plushie just *exists* in frame — on the desk, the shoulder, peeking in. The plushie itself is the through-line.
- **Reacts (a little effort):** cut to / hold up Koto at the emotional beat — you say the scary thing, Koto reacts (puppet it hiding, peeking, hopeful). Koto = the feeling the audience is having.
- **POV (more effort):** Koto voices the scared-but-hopeful counterpoint — the companion who's terrified of the timelines but wants us to make it.

**The 5 rules that keep it coherent across creators:**
1. Koto **feels the doom with you** — scared, not cynical.
2. Koto **never says "AI is fine."** Scared *and* hopeful is the register (this is the Tritium-kun guardrail — cute on-ramp, never "it's all okay").
3. Koto **roots for the researchers** — pro-us-figuring-it-out, allergic to giving up.
4. **Visual constant:** the same Koto plushie / look, every time.
5. **One tiny signature** (pick one, keep it): Koto peeks out at scary AI news → hides → peeks back with a little determined face. Wordless, repeatable, recognizable.

**Plug-and-play moments creators can copy:** doom stat drops → Koto covers eyes. Someone says "we might not make it" → Koto peeks, then sits up determined. Good news / a researcher makes a point → Koto celebrates. End of video → Koto waves from the corner.
