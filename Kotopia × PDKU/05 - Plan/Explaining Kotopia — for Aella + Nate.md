# Explaining Kotopia — for Aella + Nate

Prep for the real conversations. Both will ask "what is Kotopia" and Mara will likely show the deck ([[Koto mission file + affective synthesis (canon)]]). This gives a universal core, the "over a year with Koto" story, and two audience framings.

## The universal core (anyone, ~15 seconds)
> "Kotopia is a character-IP company built like a software company — think Sanrio, but AI-native and bilingual from day zero. Two characters, KOTO and KAIOS, that people meet through cute software that's actually live: an expression keyboard + messaging app, a multiplayer world, and an AI character with real memory continuity."

## The "over a year" story (deploy with BOTH — it's your credibility)
This is the line that separates you from "just got offered a title." It says: not a whim, not opportunistic, you were doing the work before it paid off.
> "I've been working with Koto for over a year — since before any of this was a fundable company. I'm user zero of the whole thing, I built [____], and now he's bringing me on properly as CTO."

**Fill in the blanks with your real contributions** (slot the true specifics): the editor, the tooling/DSL, content/videos in the world, whatever you actually shipped. The concreteness is what makes it land — "I built X and Y over the last year" beats "I've been involved." *(Mara: tell me the real list and I'll tighten this line.)*

## Aella version (warm — bring the whole self)
She knows you, she's heterodox, she'll get all of it. You can include the thesis and the research bridge:
> "It's a cute-software company, but built on a deliberately restrained design — anti-engagement, express-don't-surveil, no romance-bait, disclosed as AI, non-companion. Basically the opposite of the extraction-app playbook. I've been on it with Koto for over a year — user zero, built [____], and he's bringing me on as CTO. And honestly it's also the closest thing I've seen to an *aesthetic* answer to AI-doom — a version of the future people actually want to live in. That's the thread that ties into my own research."

Show her the deck freely.

## Nate version (honest, restrained — lead with the truth, not the pitch)
Do NOT lead with "we make people love AI" or "demand side of AI safety." Lead with honesty; let him poke.
> "Kotopia's a character-IP / cute-software company — I've been building it with the founder for over a year and he's bringing me on as CTO. I'll be straight with you: I don't think a cute app is AI safety. Two things I *do* think are genuinely interesting. One, the design stance is unusually restrained for a consumer AI product — no engagement-maximizing loops, no romance mode, express-not-surveil, AI-disclosed, with an actual 'what we won't build' list. Two, the technical piece. My research interests in alignment are a separate thread — I'm not going to pretend the company is the safety work."

Why this works: the "I won't pretend it's safety work" line is the single most credibility-building thing you can say to him. It signals you're not safety-washing — which is the thing he's scanning for.

## Two heads-ups for the Nate conversation
1. **The deck's language is a landmine with him.** "We build the demand side of AI safety" and "cute is how the good timeline wins attention" read to a halt/pause person as *demand generation for AI = acceleration.* If you show him the deck, get ahead of it: *"that's the investor framing — my own view is more skeptical than the deck sounds."* Better yet, **describe Kotopia to Nate rather than showing the deck**; save the deck for Aella and the suits.
2. **KAIOS's self-authored memory cuts both ways with him.** The nightly-self-authored, never-human-edited, can-reach-out-unbidden feature is technically impressive — but to Nate a consumer AI with persistent self-state and unprompted outreach can read as *mildly agentic and slightly alarming*, not just cool. So bring it as "here's something technically real we shipped," be ready to discuss its bounds (disclosed, non-companion, bounded), and don't flex it as "we gave an AI autonomy." Read his face; if it's landing as concern, pivot to the restraint stance.

## The through-line for both
Whatever framing: **over a year, user zero, built real things, now formalizing as CTO** — and *my research is a separate thread.* Keep the company and the research distinct even while explaining the company, so neither contaminates the other.
