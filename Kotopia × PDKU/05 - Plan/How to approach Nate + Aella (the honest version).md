# How to approach Nate + Aella (the honest version)

Mara's shift (2026-07-21): stop engineering a pitch; just tell Nate and Aella the situation as it actually is and **ask for advice.** This note holds why that's right and how to do it without it reading as unfocused.

## The core: ask for advice, not for a job
The honest move is also the strategically strong one. Nate and Aella meet endless pitches; far fewer people come and say *"here's my real situation, I'm trying to build a life around this, help me think."* That:
- is disarming and true (no performance to maintain),
- makes them collaborators, not targets,
- lowers the stakes so a real relationship can form — and "ask for advice" is exactly the move that *produces* offers anyway (it's how Elwyn got seen → the MIRI offer).

**The most important thing Mara wants is to be *seen* as valuable.** You don't get seen by rationalists via a confident pitch — you get seen by letting them watch how you *think*, honestly, uncertainty included. Mara's own research already does this (it labels its speculative parts). That intellectual honesty is the trait that makes someone like Nate go "oh, this person is real."

## The one discipline: name the threads, don't hand over the tangle
"Explain everything" is right — but sort it *for* them. The failure mode isn't honesty, it's dumping the unsorted braid (CTO + raise + research + book + spirituality + moving) so it reads as *unfocused*. Fix: state them as **distinct threads you're consciously holding**, and be honest you're working out how they fit.

The three threads, said cleanly:
1. **Kotopia** — becoming CTO + helping it raise (a company + a role).
2. **My own research** — alignment × spirituality; maybe a job, maybe independent, maybe a book. Doesn't need anyone's permission.
3. **The life** — basing myself here, out of Belgium, among people I'm actually aligned with. This is old, not invented now.

"I have three things going on and I'm trying to figure out which is the spine and which are side-quests" is a *strong, self-aware* sentence. It is not the same as "here's everything I want."

## Aella first (warm node — bring the whole self)
She already knows you (the haters conversation), she's heterodox, she'll get the company *and* the research *and* the spirituality without flinching. She's the safe place to say the whole thing out loud. Ask her: what do you think, who should I talk to, and *how does someone actually base themselves here* — she may have concrete answers (network, who's hiring, logistics). She can both sort you and intro you.

## Nate (newer, cleaner — lead with the thinking)
Don't hand him the life-plan. Have a real conversation about the *thinking* — your research, honestly framed, including what you're unsure of. Let him see how you reason. If it goes well, "I'm trying to work out whether there's a version of this I could do as work" can surface naturally, or you just ask his advice too.
- **Concrete signal:** he hired Elwyn for *comms / short-video production.* That means MIRI is **actively hiring for the exact comms/media capability you also have** — the door isn't hypothetical, and Nate values that skillset *right now.*
- Keep Kotopia as context, not the pitch (see [[Which door for which person]] / [[Pitching Nate Soares (MIRI)]] for why the raise doesn't go in his room).

## On the hypehouse / Elwyn (the sad part)
Elwyn taking the MIRI job → he's off the hypehouse. That's a real loss of a vehicle you wanted in on. But the hypehouse was always contingent on Elwyn driving it; losing that specific vehicle doesn't lose the underlying thing — being *here*, among these people, making things. Kotopia-as-continuation-layer is arguably a stronger version of the same wish. Other vehicles will come.

## Two practical anchors
- **Jobs aren't just income — they're how you get to stay.** Moving Belgium→US has real work-authorization logistics; a role (MIRI, a US-based Kotopia, or a funded project) is often what actually makes "live here" legal and durable. That's a concrete reason the job threads matter, not just the research/company ones.
- **Don't over-index on one person's yes/no.** You have multiple threads precisely so no single person is a gate. If Nate isn't the door, Aella might be, or the company, or the book. Hold them loosely.

## Do-not
- Don't present as more figured-out than you are — with these people, honest uncertainty is a *strength.*
- Don't let "now is the moment" rush you into overclaiming.
- Don't bury the life-goal behind the professional one; "I'm trying to base myself here" is a normal, helpable thing to say.
