# When it feels now-or-never

For the anxious moment before walking up to them. Re-read this then. (Mara, 2026-07-21)

## It's not now-or-never
You spent weeks building an *ongoing* thing — the CTO role, a reason to come back, your own research. The whole point is that this is a **start, not a one-shot.** It doesn't have to be now-or-never. It just has to be *started.*

## You're already in the door (not starting from zero)
- **Aella** knows you — the haters conversation. A follow-up is not a cold open.
- **Azmyth** knows you — you talked about psychosis. That's a *real, vulnerable* conversation. You already do the hard version of connecting; that's the rare skill.
- **Nate** has seen you think, in Q&As.
- You're a **fellow.** You have a legitimate place here. You are not imposing.

The "I'd be barging in as a nobody" story is factually wrong.

## Shrink the step
- **Goal this month = start a thread with a next step.** NOT land anything. "They know I exist, know what I'm working on, and there's a reason to talk again" = win.
- **Ask advice, not pitch.** "Can I ask your advice about something" needs zero performance, is almost impossible to refuse, flatters them. It's the emotionally-doable version.
- **Easy → hard.** Aella first (she knows you). Momentum there → then Nate. Don't do the scariest one first.

## For the actual moment
- **Continuation, not cold open:** "hey — I'm the one who talked to you about the haters thing—"
- **Pre-load your first two sentences** so you're not improvising while scared. Preparation is one of the few things that actually lowers fear — that's what the openers + deck are *for.*
- **Low-pressure setting:** a walk, a meal, after a talk. Not a formal pitch moment.
- **Written first if needed:** a two-line message opens the door and makes the in-person easier.

## "Back soon, with a plan" = achievable without a yes
1. Had a real conversation with Aella + Nate.
2. Have a concrete reason to return (CTO role / research thread / "stay in touch").
3. Know the next three steps.
Knowing you'll be back comes from *starting relationships with a next move* — not from closing a deal in 10 days.

## Gentle
If the anxiety is heavier than nerves, go at a sustainable pace and lean on the kind people here. You don't have to do it all at once, or perfectly. The anticipation is almost always worse than the act, and the first one is the hardest — it gets easier right after.
