# Grant fundability notes

Verified 2026-07-18. What each funder actually looks for + the documented rejection reasons. Feeds [[Fundable — the grant-ready shape]]. Flag: funder pages change; several internals (SFF math, LTFF questions) aren't public and are secondary-sourced.

## FLI — Digital Media Accelerator (most relevant)
- Funds **creators raising AI-risk/safety awareness for new audiences** (YouTube/TikTok/newsletter/podcast).
- **Two hard bars:** (1) creators **who already have a following**; (2) will cover AI safety **on an ongoing basis, integrated into regular output** — not a one-off.
- Rolling; apply via form; contact **maggie@futureoflife.org**. High volume, little feedback.
- Grant size + rubric **unpublished** — treat the two bars as the de facto filter.

## FLI — Futures program (bigger, research/institutional)
- **No unsolicited apps** for main tracks — periodic RFPs/contests/fellowships. (Accelerator is the rolling exception.)
- Project grants **$100k–$500k / 2–3 yrs**; center grants $500k–$1.5M; some RFP-specific $15k micro-grants. **Max 15% overhead.** No compute/equipment.
- **Biggest red flag: "fishing for money"** — recasting an existing program to appear to fit the theme **= rejected.** Lens is "impact per dollar"; an "inappropriately high budget may harm the proposal."

## SFF — Survival & Flourishing Fund
- **S-Process** ~1–2×/yr: 4–12 **Recommenders** assign **marginal utility functions** per application; a simulation allocates.
- **Key:** the mechanism **rewards ≥1 enthusiastic champion**, not bland consensus. Pitch to excite *one* recommender.
- **Speculation Grants** (fast track): ~40 Speculators, ~$20M total, ~$400k each. **One rolling application** requests both a fast Speculation Grant *and* S-Process entry. ~1 week–14 days. *"To get money faster, provide MORE information, not less."*

## Manifund
- Post a project; **regrantors** ($5k–$50k discretionary) fund directly and **often initiate by reaching out** → networking to a regrantor matters as much as the write-up. Staff do a legitimacy screen. <1 week possible.
- **Good write-up:** description, **theory of change**, **itemized use of funds**, **minimum viable funding**, team track record.
- **Template to copy — "Making God" documentary:** positioned as **public education not art**; explicit causal chain (educate → informed constituency → policy); **track record with hard numbers** (prior "60M+ views"); measurable targets (viewership scenarios, **pre/post surveys**); **epistemic honesty** ("at most we might catalyze a spirit"). Raised ~$300k+ toward $1.6M.

## LTFF — Long-Term Future Fund
- Paperform (questions not public). Funds research, policy, **advocacy, demonstration/comms**, and **individual stipends**. Credentials not required; **demonstrated skill + concrete ToC are.**
- **Comms precedent: Rob Miles $121,575** (1-yr stipend for AI-safety videos/podcasts).
- Hits-based: fund when **one manager is very excited.**
- **Published rejection reasons:** most common = **"expected outcomes don't justify expenses"**; "vague appeals to important topics without concrete plans"; weak specific proposals on hot topics. Usually **no feedback.**

## Theory of change (comms) — what "strong" means
Explicit **output → outcome → impact**, each link's assumptions stated. **Name the audience precisely** and say *why reaching them reduces risk* (not "raise awareness"). Measure **reach + engagement + downstream behavior** with a baseline + survey/attribution. Pure reach = vanity metric.

## Cross-funder red flags (creative/media)
1. No ToC / vague "awareness" — **top killer.**
2. Vanity metrics (reach with no outcome link).
3. **"Diluted epistemics" / mass-movement risk** — post-FTX wariness of outreach that dilutes epistemics or drives AI hype. **A cute mascot IP is especially exposed** — can read as trivializing or capabilities-adjacent.
4. Optimizing **persuasion over truth** (a "most convincing argument" prize was called "epistemically awful").
5. "Fishing for money" (FLI).
6. **Reputational risk** — anything embarrassing if screenshotted. **← the crypto footprint.**
7. Unclear ask / budget mismatch.
