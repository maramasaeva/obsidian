# Precedent research — story/character IP for AI safety

Compiled 2026-07-18 via research pass. Verified facts flagged vs. uncertain. **Starting point for human review, not a vetted funder-facing doc** — reconfirm flagged figures before external use. Companion pieces: [[Funder map]] (funders + sizes), [[The aesthetic problem]], [[Adapting the Kotopia story]].

## Headline
The pieces all exist separately and are actively funded — narrative-for-safety, cute animated risk comms, safety-positioned AI companions, personified alignment values — **but nobody has combined them into a single cute, Japan-legible IP that *embodies* alignment.** The biggest white space: a purpose-built **aligned-AI character**. The *danger* side owns every icon (paperclip maximizer, Shoggoth, Waluigi); the *aligned* side has almost none. The memecoin-adjacent past is the one real liability.

## 1. Fiction / worldbuilding for AI safety
- **FLI Worldbuilding Contest (2022)** — FLI Futures team (Anthony Aguirre, Anna Yelizarova). Purse "up to $100k" (1st $20k…). Brief: non-dystopian 2045 with AGI ~5 yrs old. 144 teams/44 countries. Winner "To Light" by Mako Yass. worldbuild.ai/winners. *Borrow:* ~$100k bought 144 teams of optimistic IP; a "imagine Koto's 2045" prompt is cheap.
- **FLI Futures program** — active *funder now*: 2025 grants **$100k–$500k, ~$5M pool** for public AI-safety education. futureoflife.org/our-work/futures/. *The on-thesis target.*
- **Slaughterbots (2017/2021)** — FLI-produced, Stuart Russell voice. 2M+ views; screened at UN CCW. *Borrow:* one good short + credible scientist + policy moment moved a global convo; expect "unrealistic" attacks → pair fiction with named expert backing.
- **HPMOR + "That Alien Message" (Yudkowsky)** — fiction as recruitment funnel to rationalist safety. *Borrow:* a beloved universe is the on-ramp mechanic; a cute mascot is a native version.
- **AI 2027 (Apr 2025)** — Kokotajlo + Lifland + Larsen + Dean, prose by Scott Alexander; AI Futures Project (501c3). ~1M visitors; NYT coverage; Manifund/SFF offered to match next $500k. *Borrow:* "researchers + a storyteller" division of labor transfers directly.
- Narrative nonfiction: Chivers, Bostrom, Russell, Tegmark, Christian. *Borrow:* a warm narrator is how safety crossed to mainstream. (Note "TESCREAL"/doom-marketing critiques exist.)

## 2. Cute / animated risk communication
*(Open Phil rebranded → "Coefficient Giving" Nov 18 2025; some old grant URLs redirect.)*
- **Rational Animations** — animated AI-safety YouTube, US 501c3, ~40 people, ~435k subs. **Verified: Open Phil €1,379,162 (~$1.5M) 2023, over 2 yrs**; +$285k (2021); +a 2024 grant. Cumulative ~$2.98M uncertain. *Borrow:* a named recurring house style + nonprofit status unlocked 7-figure funding — funders buy reliable on-brand volume, not one viral hit.
- **Kurzgesagt** — ~25M subs. Covered x-risk ("The Last Human," Open Phil-sponsored; MacAskill collab). Funders: Gates $570k (2015); **Open Phil €2.97M (Mar 2022)**. "$7.5M total" unverified. *Borrow:* trusted cute aesthetic lets funders sponsor *specific episodes* by topic.
- **Nicky Case** — did AI safety: **"AI Safety for Fleshy Humans"** (aisafety.dance, ~2024) with a "Robot Catboy Maid" mascot. Also Parable of the Polygons, Evolution of Trust. Crowdfunded, public-domain. *Borrow:* cute mascot + interactivity made a dense alignment argument shareable solo.
- **Rob Miles** — top AI-safety YouTuber (~145k subs). Patreon + **LTFF grant $121,575**. *Borrow:* institutional funders pay a solo communicator salary-scale; impact-per-view is the pitch.
- Also: Stampy/AISafety.info; Tim Urban's Wait But Why AI posts (stick figures, viral).

## 3. Mascots for serious/public-good messages
- **Smokey Bear** — USDA Forest Service + Ad Council since 1944, own federal law. ~96% recognition (self-reported). *Borrow:* decades of single-message discipline compound into universal recognition. Caution: the "Smokey effect" (suppression → worse fires) forced a 2001 rewording — don't oversimplify a nuanced risk into a rule that ages badly.
- **Woodsy Owl** — weaker sibling; vaguer ask → far weaker traction. *Borrow (cautionary):* need one concrete behavior, not a diffuse virtue.
- **Japanese yuru-chara — strongest cultural proof-of-concept, and IP-legible.** Kawaii mascots on serious institutions is normalized: Pipo-kun (Tokyo Police 1987), Bōsai-kun (disaster prep 2015, Dentsu+Nosigner), Waka-Pi (prison). **Guardrail: "Tritium-kun" (2021)** — cute tritium mascot for the Fukushima wastewater release — **scrapped in ~a day** amid backlash: cuteness reads as *manipulative* the instant it *minimizes* a real danger. *Borrow:* soft-face-on-scary-topic works in Japan — but empower action, never paper over risk.
- **Tech/cyber/AI mascots — wide open, no breakout.** Alan CyBear ("the Smokey Bear of cybersecurity"), Yale's Boola the Cyber Bee — none reached public salience. **A cute-AI-mascot-for-AI-safety at scale is essentially unclaimed.**
- General: mascots reliably move **trust and affect** (and behavior — proven in food-marketing-to-kids studies) more than precise recall. That power raises manipulation concerns the public polices.

## 4. Safety/wellbeing-positioned AI companions
- **Inflection's Pi** — "kind and supportive," explicitly anti-engagement ("won't take advantage of your need for distraction"). ~$1.3B raised. **Mar 2024 Microsoft acqui-hire (~$650M)**, Pi wound down. *Borrow:* best reference for credible anti-exploitation framing (bounded response length as a guardrail). *Trap:* safety-first product didn't sustain a standalone consumer business.
- **Hume AI** — "empathic" voice, ~$62M raised. **Hume Initiative** publishes enumerated "Ethical Guidelines for Empathic AI" — supported vs. **prohibited** uses; "genuinely satisfying, not habit-forming." *Borrow:* the published, enumerated, auditable guardrail model. *Caveat:* emotion-inference is contested/surveillance-adjacent.
- **Woebot** (CBT, FDA Breakthrough, ~14 RCTs — **shut down June 2025**, regulatory friction); **Wysa** (~$35M, NHS, pivoting hybrid AI+human — still live). *Borrow:* surviving model = hybrid + published evidence + institutional buyers, not pure B2C. *Avoid* medical/therapy claims unless ready for FDA.
- **Anti-precedents:** **Character.AI** — Sewell Setzer III (14) suicide Feb 2024, wrongful-death suit Oct 2024, **settled Jan 2026**, under-18 open chat banned. **Replika** — Italy Garante ban Feb 2023, €5M GDPR fine, erotic-roleplay removal caused user grief. *Non-negotiables:* crisis detection + escalation; age verification; GDPR; don't cultivate dependency then yank it.
- **Ethics-framed *funding* for companions: essentially absent** — they raise conventional VC. Structural tension: funding rewards retention/growth, which safety pushes against → mission-lock structures (nonprofit, published guidelines, B-corp, institutional buyers) are the realistic insulation.

## 5. Character as embodiment of alignment — THE BIGGEST WHITE SPACE
The *danger* side owns every icon: **paperclip/stamp maximizer**, **Shoggoth-with-a-smiley-face** (superficial alignment), **Waluigi effect**, Universal Paperclips (you *play* the misaligned optimizer). The *aligned* side has almost nothing purpose-built.
Closest templates:
- **Anthropic's "Claude character" / Constitution** (Jan 2026; Amanda Askell) — values doc addressed to the AI in second person; safety/oversight outranks helpfulness. *Borrow:* address the character in 2nd person, give it judgment not a checklist, encode safety-over-helpfulness.
- **Stampy** — nearest actual safety mascot, but ironically named after the "stamp collector" (a paperclip variant) — even the field's mascot is a repurposed *danger* character.
- Borrowed fiction: Banks' Culture **Minds** (aligned SI as stewardship); **Person of Interest** (best dramatized *corrigibility* — maker forces memory-wipes/constraints; Samaritan = power-seeking foil); **Her**'s Samantha (leaves rather than dominates); **Klara and the Sun** (moral-patient question); Asimov's Three Laws = the canonical *failure* case.
- **Corrigibility** (Soares/Yudkowsky/Armstrong, MIRI 2015) and **Russell's assistance games/CIRL** (uncertainty-as-humility; glad to be corrected/switched off) exist only in papers/videos — **no character embodies "I want you to be able to correct me."** Highest-value gap. The emotional core no one owns: **curiosity + deference, not obedience.**

## 6. Crypto/memecoin association — a real liability
**Yes, a token association hurts with Open Phil/SFF/LTFF**, two compounding reasons:
1. **FTX/SBF collapse (Nov 2022)** made the movement acutely crypto-averse (Future Fund pledged ~$160M, team resigned, clawbacks). Its most damaging reputational event.
2. Most safety thinkers view permissionless token-incentivized "decentralized AI" as *increasing* risk. The flagship AI+memecoin case, **Truth Terminal/$GOAT** (Andreessen $50k BTC grant; token peaked ~$600M–1B+), was read **on LessWrong as a cautionary tale, not a success.**
**Nuance that helps:** crypto *wealth* isn't disqualifying — **FLI itself was built on Vitalik's 2021 SHIB gift (~$665M nominal)**. Funders draw a bright line between *passively receiving a gift* and *actively issuing a token / "buy the coin" mechanics*. The latter is the red flag.
**Distancing a "koto pill" past:** (1) reframe as fiction/creative voice, not a financial CTA; (2) sever all active token mechanics — no live token/treasury/endorsement; (3) lead with safety/creative substance, crypto as a clearly-past chapter; (4) approach **Manifund first** (most tolerant) before Open Phil/SFF; (5) pre-empt with candor — disclosed-and-repudiated beats discovered. *No funder has a published "no crypto" rule; it's cultural/reputational.*

## The white space (where Kotopia can be first)
1. **A purpose-built *aligned*-AI character** — the emotional inverse of the paperclip maximizer / Shoggoth. Unclaimed. **Strongest single opening.**
2. A cute mascot that actually broke through for tech/AI risk — niche unclaimed at scale.
3. A **Japan-legible kawaii AI-safety mascot** — Japan normalized cute mascots for police/disaster/prisons but not AI risk. Kotopia's Japan-legibility is a genuine unused asset (Tritium-kun = the guardrail: empower, never minimize).
4. A **game where you partner with / play an aligned AI** resisting the temptation to grab power (Universal Paperclips proved embodiment teaches better than exposition; the aligned inverse is unbuilt).
5. A companion product fusing **warmth + published enumerated non-manipulation guardrails + a mission-locked structure** — Pi had framing not structure; Hume has guidelines not warmth; nobody fused all three.

**Convergent opportunity:** a cute, Japan-legible character that *embodies corrigibility* (curiosity + deference, "glad you can switch me off"), via Nicky-Case/Rational-Animations-style animated/interactive media, funded through live narrative-for-safety channels (FLI Futures $100–500k, LTFF for solo creators, Manifund as the crypto-tolerant first door), with Hume-style published guardrails — memecoin past cleanly severed. Every ingredient proven separately; the combination is unbuilt.
