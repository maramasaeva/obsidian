# Continuation layer — exact description

The concrete "what it actually is," usable word-for-word with Elwyn or a funder. Strategy/why lives in [[The continuation-layer play]].

**One sentence:** the continuation layer is the *persistent infrastructure* — shared IP, production, housing, funding, and distribution — that turns disposable 30-day creator sprints into an ongoing, fundable content institution; **Kotopia is its creative substrate**, the universe that gives it identity and a reason to cohere.

**The problem it solves (concretely):** PDKU is 30 days. A creator spends it building skills, audience, momentum — then the program ends and there's *nothing.* No shared home, no IP to keep making, no production support, no ongoing funding. Momentum dies, or the creator solo-hustles their own grant (the Frame fellow who went 20k→200k views then negotiated a year of FLI money *alone*). Every cohort, the value evaporates and funders re-fund a sprint from scratch.

**The five concrete things Kotopia-as-continuation-layer provides:**
1. **A shared universe to create in** — characters, canon, aesthetic. Creators aren't starting a personal brand from zero; they make canon in a world that already has fans and inherit its audience. *(Writers'-room / animation-studio model: many creators, one universe.)*
2. **Production infrastructure** — Koto's studio muscle (Asgard), design, tools, the game + chatbot tech, Elwyn's editing. A lone post-sprint creator has none of this.
3. **A physical + organizational home** — the hypehouse (the 15–20 place) is the body; people live and produce there.
4. **A single funding vehicle** — one fundable entity funders back (FLI/SFF/Manifund) that sustains many creators. Far more fundable than 20 separate applications: one theory of change, one measurable pipeline, one org.
5. **Distribution + audience that persists** — new creators plug into Kotopia's fanbase + the house's collective reach + the PDKU network. Distribution doesn't reset to zero each cohort.

**Who contributes what:**
- **PDKU / future cohorts / Frame** → the talent pipeline (trained creators).
- **Elwyn's hypehouse** → the physical body + org form + creator community.
- **Kotopia (Koto)** → IP/universe/aesthetic + production infra + existing audience.
- **Mara** → engineer/bridge (tools + tech + the line to safety funders) — CTO.
- **AI safety** → the fundable mission + the funders.

**What it produces:** content made *in* the Kotopia universe that simultaneously (a) grows the IP, (b) carries alignment intuitions to audiences the movement can't reach, and (c) generates the reach + attitude-shift metrics funders buy.

**The flywheel:** cohort feeds trained creators in → Kotopia gives them a home + universe → they produce → content grows the audience and carries safety → metrics attract funding + the next cohort → repeat. Momentum **compounds** instead of resetting every 30 days.

**Say it like this:** *"Right now these fellowships build creators up for 30 days and then drop them off a cliff. We're building the thing that catches them — a house where they live and produce inside one universe, funded as one org, so momentum compounds instead of dying. Kotopia is the universe that makes it cohere; the house is the body; AI safety is what funders fund."*
