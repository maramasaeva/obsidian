# One-sentence pitch — locked

The one-liner carries ONE idea. The funnels (keyboard, game, chatbot, merch) and the in-group signifying are the *proof underneath* — what you say next, not the sentence. Sharpening baked into all of these: Kotopia doesn't *aggregate* people who already care — it's how they *come* to care (convert, don't collect). (2026-07-21)

## PRIMARY (locked into the deck hero)
> **The Hello Kitty of AI safety — a character you fall for first, and understand later.**

Warm, memorable, instantly legible. For Aella, FLI, anyone who gets the reference. The sticky one.

## Alternates (swap in per room — one word and I'll change the deck)
- **The emotional one** (when you want them to *feel* it): *"Kotopia is somewhere to belong for the generation that can't stop loving AI and can't stop fearing it."*
- **The thesis one** (funders / the mechanism + payoff): *"Kotopia turns a generation's hatred of AI into a character worth loving — and a future worth wanting."*

## The wider bench (by what they lead with)
- *Character:* "Kotopia is a cute AI you fall in love with, and fall into AI safety through." · "…a character that makes caring about AI feel like belonging, not homework."
- *Belonging:* "…a home, an aesthetic, and a character for the AI-native generation — built around something to love instead of something to dread."
- *Counter-image:* "Kotopia is the counter-image to AI doom: an AI cute and honest enough that you'd actually want it around."
- *Product-funnel:* "Kotopia is cute software — a keyboard, a world, an AI companion — that leaves you caring about AI safety."
- *Edge / in-group:* "Kotopia is the aesthetic and the in-group for people scared enough of AI to take it seriously — and online enough to make it a scene."

## CONSUMER register — the "everyone else" door (NEVER say AI)
Per Koto's three doors: for the public, no AI, no safety — just cute + belonging + calm.
- **"Kotopia is a cute messaging app where you actually belong."**
- "Kotopia is a little world — a keyboard, a game, a companion — that feels calm instead of exhausting."
- "meet Koto: the cutest character on your phone, and a whole world behind them."
- "Kotopia is a character you'll want everywhere — your keyboard, your chats, your shelf."

## Which register when
- **Safety-aware** (primary + alternates): Aella, FLI, believers, the deck.
- **Consumer** (never-say-AI): app store, the public, normie on-ramp. The lore + safety stay subtext. → [[Koto mission file + affective synthesis (canon)]]
