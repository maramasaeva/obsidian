# Can MIRI adopt Kotopia? (no — and who fits instead)

Mara floated it (2026-07-21). Honest verdict: **MIRI adopting Kotopia is a category error** — not because Kotopia is bad, but because MIRI is arguably the *single worst-fit org* in the whole landscape for it. Don't pitch this to Nate; it would read as not-getting-what-MIRI-is and cost credibility.

## Why it doesn't work
1. **Wrong kind of org.** MIRI is a research + advocacy **nonprofit** (501c3), not a studio, incubator, VC, or consumer-product company. There's no mechanism by which it "adopts" a Delaware C-corp's character IP. Structurally it just isn't a thing MIRI does.
2. **The deep conflict.** MIRI's message is "superintelligence on the current path kills everyone; the sane move is to *stop building.*" A cute consumer AI that people *love* and get attached to is, from MIRI's seat, closer to **the problem** than the solution — it normalizes AI, grows adoption, makes AI feel friendly and safe. And they think doom is *correct and under-weighted*, not a marketing problem to be softened. "Make the future feel lovable" ≈ the opposite of their goal. (Anti-smoking org + cute cigarette mascot.)
3. **Reputational poison for them.** MIRI guards its identity as the serious, uncompromising voice. A cute, crypto-adjacent (the **$koto memecoin**!) consumer startup attaching to their name is exactly the reputational risk they avoid.
4. They're **not a grantmaker** anyway (established earlier).

## The instinct is right, the target is wrong
The underlying wish — *a serious safety org backs / legitimizes the comms mission* (the (b) fork in [[Which door for which person]]) — is valid. Redirect it to orgs that actually fund creative/media safety comms:
- **FLI (Future of Life)** — the real fit. Funds media/creative work, ran the Worldbuilding Contest, has a digital-media accelerator. "Counter-image to doom" is squarely in their lane.
- **Rational Animations-type outfits** — Open-Phil-funded animation *for* safety; a precedent, maybe a collaborator.
- **Manifund regrantors / creative-comms grants** — for a specific scoped output.

## The ONE real MIRI angle (different thing entirely)
MIRI **hiring comms/media talent** is real — they just hired Elwyn for short-video/production. That's MIRI wanting *your skills*, not adopting *Kotopia*. Keep these totally separate: "MIRI might hire a comms person like me" = plausible; "MIRI adopts Kotopia" = no. Don't let the second contaminate the first when you talk to Nate.
