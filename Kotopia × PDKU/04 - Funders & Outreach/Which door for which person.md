# Which door for which person

The mistake to avoid: treating "Kotopia route" as one pitch to one kind of person. It's **different instruments to different rooms**, and the named connectors (Aella, Ronny, Nate, Dara) are NOT interchangeable. Built off [[Koto mission file + affective synthesis (canon)]].

## The instrument split (this is the big one)
- **Kotopia's ask is a SAFE** — an *equity* raise. VC/angel instrument.
- **Safety grant funders** (FLI / SFF / LTFF / Manifund) mostly **do not buy equity in a for-profit C-corp.** They fund nonprofits or specific charitable/comms outputs. FLI's media grants *occasionally* fund a specific for-profit comms output, but SFF/LTFF generally won't touch equity.
- **Therefore:** safety-world people are mostly **(a) connectors to angels** or **(b) sources of a comms grant for a specific output** — *not buyers of this SAFE.* Don't pitch the SAFE to a grantmaker or a grant to an angel. → keep the two tracks clean.

| **Ronny / Dara** | Safety-adjacent, networked | Depends — probe whether they're angel-connectors or grant-world first | Intro *or* grant lead, once you know which |
| **FLI / SFF / Manifund** | Grantmakers | A specific fundable *comms output* (the counter-image at cadence), not the company | A grant for that output, structured so it's not "buy equity" |

## Why Nate is the wrong first Kotopia-investment node (important)
- He co-wrote *If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies*. A cute consumer-AI company that grows adoption/attachment is close to the thing he thinks shouldn't be built at all — *however* clean the safety spec.
- The deck's own words — **"demand side of AI safety," "cute is how the good timeline wins attention"** — read to a pause-advocate as *demand generation for AI = acceleration.* Landmine in his room; fine in a VC's or Aella's.
- The clean safety spec (anti-engagement, no romance, disclosure, crisis pipeline, non-companion) answers the *Character.AI/Replika* objection but **not** his deeper one (lovable AI at all → normalization). Don't expect the spec to convert him.
- **So:** with Nate, keep Kotopia as the *counter-image / reach* artifact (comms), not an investment ask. Everything else about him lives in [[Pitching Nate Soares (MIRI)]].

## The goal fork Mara has to name
Two goals pull different directions with the hardcore crowd:
1. **Be taken seriously as an alignment thinker / get MIRI-type cred** → lead with *research*; Kotopia can *undercut* this (reads as "startup founder," not "thinker").
2. **Build a funded company + a real role** → Kotopia is the vehicle; the CTO offer is exactly that.
Decide which you want *from Nate specifically* before that conversation — they're not the same play.
