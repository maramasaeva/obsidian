# Lightcone Commons — how to apply

lightconecommons.com. NOT a club or residency — a **philanthropy-coordination / grantmaking platform** run by Lightcone Infrastructure (Oliver Habryka's org — the people behind LessWrong *and Lighthaven*, i.e. the campus PDKU is on). Very warm network. (2026-07-23)

## What it is (so you frame it right)
- A **funding channel.** This is the **grant** route, not the investor/equity route → belongs in the "an org backs the safety-comms mission" fork, same category as FLI. **Do not** confuse it with the Kotopia SAFE round. See [[Which door for which person]].
- They fund a **scoped safety-comms output**, not "please fund my cute startup."
- Applicants: online applications, editable/lightweight, rolling deadlines, quarterly cycles. **Next deadline looked like ~Aug 23, 2026.** For-profits OK, no 501c3 needed.
- Funders contact: habryka@lightconeinfrastructure.com. The "invite by an organizer" gate is probably the **evaluator** role, not the applicant one.

## How to go about it
1. **Confirm the gate in person.** You're on their campus — ask a Lighthaven organizer whether applicants just apply or need an invite. Fastest, and it's a warm touch, not guessing.
2. **Lock the frame before writing:** this is the *grant/comms* thread. Scoped output + audience + metrics. NOT equity, NOT "fund my company."
3. **Write the application around a concrete deliverable**, e.g. "produce the counter-image to AI-doom at cadence, seed the character, pilot reach metrics" — tie it to the wedge once you've picked it ([[The wedge — one product, one need (Aella's advice)]]).
4. **Disclose cleanly, don't get discovered.** These are DD-savvy rationalist-adjacent people — name the $koto memecoin and the C-corp structure yourself, framed honestly. Disclosed > discovered.
5. **Submit before the deadline**, but it's rolling/quarterly → **not now-or-never.** A strong scoped application beats a rushed one.

## Why it's worth doing
An organizer being one hallway away means you can just *ask how it works* instead of guessing — and it puts a real funding route in the same building as you before the month ends.
