## Read the room: who Nate is (and why the research is high-stakes with him)
- President of MIRI; co-wrote *If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies*. Position: superintelligence on the current trajectory kills everyone, alignment is **unsolved and we're not close**, force/specification isn't working — **and that's precisely why we die.**
- Mathematically rigorous, and his epistemic identity is built *against* "feelings as evidence," spirituality, and anything that sounds like it makes the danger smaller.
- **This is the crux:** Mara's central thesis — *alignment is resonance, not optimization; force fails; we need feeling/different modes of knowing in the room* — is close to the **opposite** of Nate's core view. To him, the lethal fact is precisely that a superintelligence *won't* resonate with us — it will optimize, and expecting relationship is the wishful anthropomorphizing that gets everyone killed. Walk in with "we should stop forcing and start resonating" and he files it under both "woo" *and* "the comforting story that makes people underestimate the danger" — the thing he fights hardest.
- So the game is: find the parts of your research that **survive Nate**, lead with those, and keep the landmines out of the room. Not because the rest is worthless — because burning credibility early means the real bridges never get heard.

## The one meta-move: earn it, then offer
Your cosmotechnics script already engages Scott Alexander's Moloch seriously and *labels its own speculative status* ("a reframing, not a technical proposal; the question the video is asking, not answering"). **That intellectual honesty is your single biggest asset with Nate.** Open by showing you take the hard, lethal, unsolved version of the problem seriously — then offer the angle as one peer to another. Overclaim any of it as "the answer" and the door shuts.

## The angles, ranked by Nate-survivability

### Angle 1 — "Culture is training data. I shape what the models eat." (STRONGEST)
This is your hyperstition idea with the woo stripped off, and it's **mechanistically real**, not metaphysical. Your own note says it plainly: *"if the next general intelligence is trained on the corpus of human-generated internet content — and it is — then every post is a training example."*
- **The defensible version:** the AI-content saturating the internet is mostly hype or doom/adversarial-AI roleplay. Both poison the training distribution — misaligned-AI personas and self-fulfilling "AI will betray you" priors get learned. Someone who makes culture *deliberately* is operating a real lever on what future models internalize.
- **Why it lands with Nate:** no metaphysics required, it's a genuine and discussed concern, and it's exactly MIRI's comms-era problem (shape the narrative environment) with a technical edge (training-data hygiene / narrative poisoning).
- **The ask it supports:** comms/media work, or research into how AI-narratives in training corpora shape model behavior. This is the door.

### Angle 2 — "I see your central failure mode from the outside, and I can name it to people who'll never read the paper." (STRONG)
Your Jullien bridge independently arrives at MIRI's oldest argument: *"the model learns to satisfy the specification without satisfying the intent."* That **is** specification-gaming / Goodhart / "you can't write down human values" — MIRI's core thesis for 15 years, reached from a completely different tradition.
- **The move:** don't offer resonance as the *fix* (he rejects it). Offer that you **see the specification-and-force failure clearly** and can articulate it, vividly, to audiences that would never touch a Goodhart paper. Value = diagnosis + reach, not a rival solution.
- **Why it lands:** you're agreeing with his diagnosis, in his favor, and bringing a translation capability he lacks.

### Angle 3 — "The field is invisible to entire populations. I'm native to them." (STRONG)
Reframe your "different thinkers" manifesto as **instrumental reach, not epistemics.** MIRI pivoted to raising the alarm broadly; it reaches rationalists and EAs and almost no one else. The queer, artistic, spiritual, neurodivergent, non-Western millions are forming their AI relationship *right now*, inside their own frameworks, and the safety field is invisible to them. You speak those languages natively and can carry the *real* danger-message there.
- **Why it lands:** true, unfakeable, and requires **zero** metaphysics from Nate — just that those audiences exist and matter.
- **Critical:** pitch this as *reach/communication*, NOT as "feeling is a valid way of knowing about alignment." The second is a head-on collision with his epistemics (see guardrails).

### Angle 4 — the cosmotechnics reframe itself (HIGH-RISK, only if he bites)
The resonance-vs-optimization argument, offered as an intellectual gift between peers — *translated into his vocabulary* (specification brittleness, the wrong objective, propensity vs. imposed target) and dropped the instant it reads as woo. Only deploy if Angles 1–3 have bought you real credibility and he's leaning in. Never lead with it.

## Guardrails — keep these OUT of the Nate room
Not because they're wrong for you — because with *him specifically* they burn the credibility the bridges need. This is triage, not a verdict on the work.
- **Manifestation / time-sorcery / "the zero built this through me" / spirituality-as-literally-efficacious** — radioactive with Nate. Hold them as *your* practice/aesthetic, never as claims about how the world works.
- **"Feelings/feeling-response are a valid way of knowing about alignment"** — direct collision with his epistemics. Reframe as reach (Angle 3), not epistemology.
- **"Resonance replaces optimization" / "alignment isn't really a specification problem"** — he thinks the optimization *is* the lethal fact. Don't frame resonance as the reassuring answer.
- **Anything that implies the danger is therefore smaller.** The one unrecoverable move with Nate is sounding like "so it's going to be okay."

## Honest bottom line
Your work is real and, in the specification-brittleness convergence, genuinely sharp. But your *thesis* and Nate's *worldview* point opposite directions. The winning play isn't to convert him — it's to lead with the two or three things that are true in **both** frames (culture-as-training-data, specification-failure-seen-from-outside, reach into invisible populations), prove you take the lethal version seriously, and let the deeper reframe stay a between-peers offering. Aim for a lasting contact / a small collaboration / a look at MIRI's comms needs — not "hire me as a research fellow." A held-open door beats a big ask refused.
