# The Aella conversation — built out

The full version of the one that matters most. Advice/convo, never pitch. Aella already knows you (haters convo) AND has real FLI ties — so this can naturally open the FLI door, *if she offers it.* Say everything in your own voice; these are the shape + lines to lean on. (2026-07-21)

## The golden rule for this whole conversation
**Say one thread, then STOP and let her react.** Anxiety will push you to monologue the whole plan in one breath — don't. The best outcome isn't that you impressed her; it's that she got *interested and started pulling the thread herself.* Leave gaps for her to step into.

## Opener (pick by setting)
- Sit-down: *"can i steal you before the month's over? i'm at a fork, and you're one of the few people whose read i actually trust."*
- Casual/walking: *"can i think out loud at you for a sec? i'm trying to figure something out."*
- Message-first (if walking up is too much): two lines — *"before the month ends i'd love your read on something i'm stuck on — got 15 min at some point?"*

## The flow (thread → pause → thread)
1. **Bridge from what she knows.** *"you know koto, right? have u heard about kotopia, his creative project?"*
2. **The company + what Kotopia is.** Over a year with Koto → now CTO. This is where she needs ~20 sec of "what *is* this" — do the **character-first** explanation below, then: *"and in the last few weeks it hit me it might be bigger than a company."* → *pause.*
3. **The thesis.** *"everyone hates AI right now, for good reasons — art, jobs, attention. and the safety world can't reach any of those people. i keep thinking koto could turn that hate into actual communication. a trojan horse. the creative face of the safe-AI thing."* → *pause, let her react — she'll have takes.*
4. **Research, kept separate.** *"and separately i've got my own research — the resonance-vs-optimization stuff — that i don't know what to do with yet. book, job, dunno."*
5. **The real thing.** *"and honestly the thing under all of it: i want to be here. i don't want to go back to belgium, working a corporate job while doing kotopia and alignment shit on the side, i want to be part of this and do important work. im. literally just taking people's jobs rn and i hate it. i want to be back in that startup environment, dynamic, u never know whats gna happen, theres this kind of risk to it that makes it worth living for"*
6. **The ask (open).** *"so that's the fork. i don't know how to weight these or make any of it real. if you were me, what would you chase first? koto has beenhandling the funding shit mostly and he said there are ppl interested in japan, but we both want this to take off in the us too, since he's based in NY and i really want to be in the US too. "*

## The little Kotopia explanation (character-first — do it at step 2)
**What to focus on: CHARACTER. Not the app, not the engine, not the safety spec.** Character is the instant click, it's true, it's what you find natural — and it's the on-ramp to the whole thesis. Tech is *one supporting line*; safety is the thesis that comes *after*, not the definition.

**Calibrate first** (Aella may already know Koto — he vouched for you to her): *"you know koto's project, kotopia, right?"* → if yes, one line; if no, the full version.

- **One-liner (she knows Koto):** *"it's basically a character IP — like a hello kitty or labubu — but the character's an AI, built to be the opposite of the creepy extractive stuff. i'm coming on as CTO."*
- **Full (~20-30s):** *"you know character IP — hatsune miku, labubu, hello kitty — characters young people fall for and want everywhere? it's that, except the character's actually an AI, and the whole thing's built to be the opposite of the extractive attention stuff: calm, honest, doesn't spy on you. and it's real, not a deck — an app that just hit the app store, a game, a chatbot with real memory. koto's the mouthless mascot, kaios is the AI. i've built it with him for over a year."*

**The hinge into the thesis** (this is *why* character-first matters — the explanation becomes the thesis): *"and the thing that got me: young people get pulled in by character, not arguments. so what if it's the hello kitty of AI safety? the character carries the message to everyone a paper never reaches."*

**If she asks how something like this actually blows up online →** the Milady mechanism, carefully: *"kind of like milady — an aesthetic people build an identity around — but without the edgelord baggage. the edge is dread, not controversy."* Don't *lead* with Milady; it's a second-level reference, not the definition. → [[The dark twin — dread not controversy]]

## The FLI soft-landing (let it be HER idea)
Don't ask "introduce me." Ask for her read on *fit* — the intro offers itself:
- If she hasn't raised it: *"you know the FLI people, right? do you even think this is something they'd care about — the creative-front thing? i genuinely don't know if it fits."*
- If she offers an intro: *"that would honestly mean a lot. no rush — whenever feels right."*
- If she doesn't offer: leave it. You got her read, which is the win. Don't push.

## How she might push back — and your honest answers
Aella is blunt, data-minded, unshockable, allergic to bullshit. Meet every one straight:
- **"What do you actually want?"** → *"honestly? to not go back to belgium. to do this somewhere real, with people like the ones here."*
- **"Is it your company or Koto's?"** → *"koto's — he founded it, i've built with him over a year, he's bringing me on as CTO. i'm user zero."*
- **"Is it real or vapor?"** → *"shipped — app store, the world, the chatbot. real c-corp, small round. not vapor."*
- **"What about the memecoin?"** → *"koto launched it early and naive, it pumped, mostly whales extracted. he's not attached to it, we don't lean on it. i'd rather say it than have someone find it."* (disclosed > discovered)
- **"Isn't this safety-washing / naive?"** → *"maybe — that's the thing i'm most scared of. that's why the message stays honest: not 'AI is fine,' but 'you're right to be angry, here's the version built the opposite way, and here's the real danger.' the dread stays in."*
- **"Why not just do the research?"** → *"i might. they're separate threads and i can hold both. the research is mine regardless. kotopia's the vehicle that could actually reach people — and could let me stay."*
- **"CTO or researcher — which?"** → *"both, and i'm trying to work out if that's possible or if i have to choose. that's part of the fork."*

## The close (leave a thread with a next move)
- *"can i send you a couple paragraphs — or the deck — so it's not just me talking at you at a party?"*
- *"and can i follow up when i'm back? i'm trying to leave knowing this is a start, not a one-off."*

## Don't
- Don't monologue — thread, then pause.
- Don't pitch or go corporate — she'll feel it instantly.
- Don't be vague on the ask (the fork *is* the ask).
- Don't over-apologize / shrink yourself.
- Don't hide the Belgium/wanting-to-stay part — that's the helpable part.
- Don't overclaim "face of AI safety" as fact — hold it as the idea you're testing.
