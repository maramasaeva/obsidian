# The Aella + Nate conversations (advice, not pitch)

The thing to actually say — as conversation, thinking-out-loud, asking for advice. NOT a memorized script and NOT a pitch. These are the *shape* and some sample lines in Mara's voice; say them however's natural. (2026-07-21)

## The idea it's built on: Kotopia as the creative face of AI safety (for FLI)
"Face" ≠ mascot of the whole field (grandiose, won't fly). It means: the **creative/aesthetic front** — the counter-image to doom, the thing that carries the message to people who'll never read a paper, the proof you can be pro-human, anti-extraction, honest about danger *and* make something people love.
- **Why FLI specifically:** they fund creative/media safety comms — Worldbuilding Contest, digital-media accelerator, the Slaughterbots precedent. "A future worth wanting, rendered well enough that people want to live there" is literally their language.
- **The FLI ask (later):** a grant/partnership to produce the counter-image at cadence + the seeded character + pilot metrics — *not* equity (grants≠equity, see [[Which door for which person]]).
- **Discipline:** this "face of AI safety" framing is for the **Aella / FLI** track. With **Nate it's a landmine** — do NOT call a cute app "the face of AI safety" to him. See his section.

## AELLA — the advice-fork (bring the whole self)
**Register:** vulnerable, excited-but-uncertain, thinking out loud with someone whose judgment you trust. She already knows you (haters convo). You can show the deck. You can name the feelings.

**The shape:**
1. **Open — continuation + fork.** *"can i steal you before the month's over? i'm kind of at a fork and you're one of the few people whose read i actually trust."*
2. **The company thread.** Over a year with Koto, now CTO. Then the click: *"somewhere in the last few weeks it hit me it might be bigger than a company."*
3. **The thesis (turn-the-hate).** *"everyone hates AI right now, for good reasons — art, jobs, attention. and the safety world can't reach any of those people. i keep thinking koto could turn that hate into actual communication — a trojan horse. the creative face of the safe-AI thing, especially for somewhere like FLI."*
4. **The research thread (kept distinct).** *"and separately i've got my own research — the resonance-vs-optimization stuff — which i don't know what to do with yet. maybe a book, maybe a job."*
5. **The real thing underneath.** *"and honestly the thing under all of it is i want to be here. i don't want to go back to belgium."*
6. **The ask (advice, open-ended).** *"so that's the fork — i don't know how to weight these or how to make any of it real. if you were me, what would you chase first? and who should i even be talking to?"*

**What she can give:** react to the thesis, name FLI-type people / angels, advise on the CTO deal, advise on how to actually relocate/stay.

## NATE — research-first, honest, advice (NOT the company pitch)
**Register:** peer-to-peer, intellectually honest, modest about Kotopia. You've asked him things in Q&As — this is picking that up. **Lead with thinking, not the company.**

**The shape:**
1. **Open — continuation.** *"i've asked you a couple things in the Q&As — can i run something by you properly?"*
2. **The thinking first.** *"i've been coming at alignment from a weird angle — that the field's really good at the specification-and-force framing and kind of blind to how much it selects for one type of thinker and can't reach most people. i don't think that's a solution to anything — it's a reframe — but it's led me to stuff i think is real, like how much the culture we make now becomes the training data for the next models."*
3. **Kotopia — modest, honest.** *"i've also been building a thing on the side — a cute-AI company, about to be CTO — and i'll be straight, i don't think a cute app is AI safety. but the design side is unusually restrained, and i think i can reach people you guys structurally can't."*
4. **The ask (advice + the comms hook).** *"what i'm actually trying to figure out is whether there's a real version of this i could do in this world — as work, not just a fellow passing through. i saw you brought elwyn on for comms; that's the kind of thing i do too. what would you pay attention to if you were me?"*

**Do NOT with Nate:** "Kotopia is the face of AI safety" · "invest in / MIRI adopt Kotopia" · flexing KAIOS's autonomy · the deck's "demand side / win attention" language. See [[Pitching Nate Soares (MIRI)]] + [[Can MIRI adopt Kotopia (no)]].

**The one line that buys everything:** *"i'm not going to pretend the company is the safety work."* It's the opposite of safety-washing — which is what he's scanning for.
