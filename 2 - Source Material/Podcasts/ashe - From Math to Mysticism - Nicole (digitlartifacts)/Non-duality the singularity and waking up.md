## transcription

nicole draws connections between non-dual traditions and the tech concept of singularity. she names: advaita vedanta (the hindu idea of non-dual consciousness), anima mundi (world soul), and the broader pattern that material reality is illusion — the real reality is non-dual unity.

she references ken wilber's evolutionary theory: individuals develop similarly to societies. a child doesn't see themselves as separate from the mother; early indigenous societies didn't see themselves as separate from earth. then comes the teenage alienation phase — self-focused, self-interested. then ideally: return to a more collectivist understanding. ultimately: the spiritual idea that we are all one. return to the non-dual state.

she asks: are we always in a state of becoming — is that the nature of being human, this continual movement toward non-duality? or are we collectively moving closer to singularity and will all wake up to it? she doesn't claim to know. "i think that one stays in mystery."

ashe connects this to the 1960s — the raw spirit of rebellion, the authenticity, the techno-optimism of the space race. there was a pendulum swing away from that.

nicole brings in eric davis (author of *techgnosis* and *high weirdness*): the current rise of interest in the occult is unique because it's become more mainstream. but even "mainstream" is broken apart now — in the 60s there were only a few channels, so being countercultural was specific. now media is decentralized, there's no one source of truth, people get pulled in all directions, easier to be manipulated, easier to fall into conspiracy.

ashe: the epstein files validated some conspiracy theorists. alien disclosure from the white house. nicole: she went down conspiracy rabbit holes as a teenager and feels more comfortable now because of it. for people newly confronting destabilizing truths: come back to the body, regulate the nervous system.

ashe adds: there's the somatic level, but also a faith point — belief in a benevolent universe, surrender into love and kindness. without that anchor, nervous system regulation alone might not suffice.

## key points

- non-dual traditions (advaita vedanta, anima mundi) map onto tech's concept of singularity — both point toward fundamental unity.
- ken wilber: individual development mirrors collective development. child→teenager→mature adult = unity→separation→return to unity.
- the 60s/70s psychedelic spiritual opening broke people open "a little bit" — but we're in a different media environment now. no single mainstream to rebel against.
- eric davis: the current occult resurgence is unique because of media decentralization. harder to find a center, easier to be manipulated.
- conspiracy validation (epstein, alien disclosure) is destabilizing for people encountering it fresh.
- two levels of stability: somatic (body, nervous system) and faith (belief in benevolent universe, surrender).
