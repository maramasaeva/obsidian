## transcription

nicole connects synchronicity directly to the trend of manifestation — setting intentions, visualizing reality, creating your own reality. then the key line: "the tech bro version of that is hyperstitioning." ashe agrees: "it's manifestation for the tech bros." the tech-bro word for it is "agency."

nicole raises the question: how much control do we actually have over this? she notes the popular manifestation trend of manifesting superficial things — apartments, lamborghinis, material wealth. she says there's a misunderstanding of the shadow at work: if you're focused on ego desires and ego goals, it can catapult you into a difficult confrontation with that material.

her reframe: it's not as direct as "visualize things you want and they'll come." the emotional state you're looking for (security, freedom, self-worth) is what to focus on — not the physical attribute. how do you feel self-worth and security internally rather than scrambling for external things? many people reach extreme material wealth and are still looking for the self-worth.

## key points

- [[manifestation]] and [[hyperstition]] are the same operation in different registers — manifestation for spiritual circles, hyperstition for tech.
- the tech-bro word for the underlying capacity is "agency."
- shallow manifestation (material wealth) misses the shadow: ego desires can catapult you into difficult confrontation.
- the real focus should be the emotional state (security, freedom, self-worth), not the external symbol.
- question left open: how much control do we actually have over reality-shaping?
