## transcription

ashe asks: if someone's nervous system is shot to hell and they're looking for something bigger than themselves, where do they start?

nicole's answer: start with the body. tune into your nervous system, your feelings. if you're emotionally triggered, that's a sign of potent work to do. regulate your nervous system. intense physical activity helps. meditation works for some. community — finding grounded people who can co-regulate your nervous system.

on plant medicine: profoundly positive experiences, but intense. higher doses "completely one-shot your consciousness out of your body." requires community, guides, people who've been there multiple times. risks include mania, psychosis, dissociative states. she comes back to: practices that keep you in your body, strong community, support from people who've been through it.

on astrology: nicole's aunt was an astrologer. she inherited her books, taught herself astrological analysis — understanding of symbol and archetype. she sees it as a storytelling framework, a mirror, a clock: "a clock doesn't create time but it does tell you what time it is." useful for communication, understanding interpersonal dynamics, making meaning. she stresses maintaining a rational skeptical frame — not becoming fatalistic or compulsive.

on spiritual emergency: stan grof and christina grof coined the term — a spiritual awakening that starts to look like mania or psychosis. nicole has friends who had increasing synchronicity experiences and psychiatrists wanted to medicate immediately. she encouraged them to find spiritually informed therapists instead. studying lineages (tarot, astrology) gives you a language to construct meaning from intense synchronicity experiences, and a community who shares that language.

ashe names the engineering archetype explicitly — how do you introduce someone with a quantitative mind into these modalities?

nicole: there used to be confluence between mystical and scientific. carl jung approached mysticism in a measured way. it's important to hold on to skeptical rational mindset while exploring these areas.

## key points

- when overwhelmed: start with the body. nervous system regulation through exercise, breathing, community.
- plant medicine is powerful but requires guides, community, and integration work. risks are real: mania, psychosis, dissociation.
- astrology as a clock, not a cause — it tells you what time it is but doesn't create time.
- astrology as condensed psychological language — shorthand for complex emotional dynamics.
- spiritual emergency (grof): spiritual awakening that looks like psychosis to Western psychiatry. don't immediately medicalize — find spiritually informed practitioners.
- synchronicity experiences cluster around transformation. studying symbolic lineages (tarot, astrology) gives language and community to hold them.
- balance is key: hold the rational skeptical frame while exploring.
