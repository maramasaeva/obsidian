## transcription

nicole: "there is this idea that we are creating our reality as we observe it. our observation of reality actually shapes it in some way." she cites the double-slit experiment and schrödinger's cat as well-accepted examples of the interplay between observation and manifestation of reality.

where it falters: the jump from quantum observer effect to "i'm going to manifest a parking spot" — science hasn't caught up to that yet. but there is a correspondence between observation and material reality that's "really interesting and beautiful."

ashe reframes: being not just an observer but a *participator* — your conscious experience may be the reason reality is unfolding. another way to approach spirituality through the history of quantum physics and all we still don't know.

ashe compliments nicole on being grounded — she's met many people in woo circles who are "very untethered," whose cadence is affected, who've "chosen to float away." nicole feels different: grounded, using rational language. nicole responds: even staunchly rationalist people tend to fall into esoteric leanings — "a neat feature of humanity is that we just tend to have these experiences of esoteric pull." balance is key: if you hold onto anything too tightly, you get pulled too far in one direction.

## key points

- quantum observer effect (double-slit, schrödinger's cat): observation shapes reality. well-accepted in physics.
- the gap: quantum mechanics doesn't yet bridge to personal manifestation ("manifest a parking spot"), but the correspondence is real.
- observer → participator: consciousness may be constitutive of reality, not just witnessing it.
- even staunch rationalists fall into esoteric leanings — it's a natural capacity of the human mind.
- grounding matters: hold balance, don't grip too tightly in either direction.
