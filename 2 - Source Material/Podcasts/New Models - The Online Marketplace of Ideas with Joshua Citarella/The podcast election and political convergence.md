## transcription

josh on the 2024 "podcast election" and its aftermath. two key examples of the structural convergence where politician becomes content creator becomes institution:

**gavin newsom**: started his own podcast, adopted content creator memetic strategy, producing merch, posting in the style of trump. now considered the frontrunner for the democratic party. the old model — vying for guest spots on shows that reach hundreds of millions — has been inverted. politicians now become the content creators themselves.

**claudia scheinbaum** (mexico): hosts a daily livestream called the *mañanera* — quantitatively the most watched media program in the country. speaking directly to constituents.

josh: "the content creators are best at getting the message out. they're also best at fundraising. and they're also best at what we call calls to action — turning people out to canvas, protest, vote." imagine a massive CSV of a substack or podcast audience list — that's what political power looks like now.

carly on *neo-orality*: as the screen space becomes overloaded with AI slop and clickbait, people want to listen to others metabolize news with an emotional vector. a few people in conversation — this is the format that works.

julian draws the comparison to rap: "you couldn't become a successful rapper unless you are a really good influencer content creator first." the social proof model now applies to politics.

carly on *doctrine as merch*: marc andreessen's techno-optimist manifesto, alex karp writing his story — doctrine as "an idealized form of merch" that backfills objects with ideological aspiration. the material precipitate of neo-orality is still merch and books.

josh calls these creators "an accidental avant-garde for 21st century media" — incredibly potent and influential, acting out of survival instinct rather than intentional system-disruption.

## key points

- structural convergence: the politician becomes the content creator becomes the institution. old media gatekeeping model is inverted.
- gavin newsom and claudia scheinbaum as examples of politician-as-content-creator being more effective than legacy media channels.
- content creators are now best at: getting the message out, fundraising, and calls to action (canvassing, protest, voting).
- neo-orality (carly): overloaded screen space drives people to audio — listening to others metabolize news with emotional vector.
- doctrine as merch: manifestos and books as idealized merch that backfills material objects with ideology.
- the "accidental avant-garde": these creators aren't intentionally disrupting — they're acting from survival instinct toward what has the most agency.
