## transcription

josh goes off on the visitors who walked in and immediately left:

"people who walk in and be like, trump, maga, ooh — this now has majority democratic support. these channels are larger than the legacy institutions that they inherited and dedicated their lives to building. these things are now steering not just culture, but politics."

"that revulsion, that response from people who are in these elite positions within those institutions — is part of why the institutions have become literally out of touch with their political constituencies. that revulsion is the evidence of them being out of touch."

"as cultural influence has moved away from these museums and cultural institutions over to the platforms, you see a similar type of disconnect. these two things are actually mirroring each other."

"so i think part of what we're trying to do is to reflect to the art world how ugly and chaotic and dysfunctional all of society has become because our institutions have drifted out of touch with their constituencies and with their publics."

the kicker: "they built this entire system that ground artists down to where they're living at the poverty level in a society without health insurance or social democratic infrastructure. they can be upset for a few minutes."

julian on exocapitalism reaching unlikely audiences: "as soon as tucker carlson reads exocapitalism it's over... he's already haunted by demons. he's in the pipeline for it." the new york times as foil: individuals like tucker carlson and independent candidates like graham platner throw into relief how institutions that purport to hold normative left-centrism are actually out of touch with common sense.

julian mentions norway's culture ministry using the political compass to map futures — "they're in new models." and that maybe they're even holding up legacy institutions as a straw man at this point.

closing: josh grateful for the collaboration. carly notes they've been in conversation about this material for at least a decade.

## key points

- the "ew trump" reflex is not critique — it's a symptom of institutional decoupling. the revulsion is the evidence of being out of touch.
- josh's structural argument: it's not that online communities are nicer or more authentic. it's that they're structurally coupled to how people actually live, buy, identify, organize. institutions lost that coupling.
- the art world built a system that ground artists to poverty without healthcare. they can be upset for a few minutes while the exhibition shows them the world they built.
- cultural institutions and political institutions are mirroring each other's disconnect from their publics.
- exocapitalism has pipeline potential even into unlikely audiences (tucker carlson, conservative media).
- some legacy institutions may already be straw men — the actual institutional power is shifting to para-institutions and new models.
