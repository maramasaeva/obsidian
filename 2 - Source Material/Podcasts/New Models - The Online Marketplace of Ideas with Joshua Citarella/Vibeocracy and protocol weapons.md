## transcription

julian on vibeocracy (via jacqueline fent): we now live in a vibeocracy. community personality matters — DNR has a community personality, new models has one, grimes, charli xcx's brat. "the critical skill is to be able to orient people around a certain vibe and conduct an audience through it."

the exhibition includes pop-star figures (charli xcx brat flag, dorian electra dvd, grimes t-shirt) because "they above all conduct a vibe and an audience through it." this is the critical skill.

julian pushes beyond vibe-as-vibes: new models podcast guests sal hagan and daniel de la zoo are doing informational-rhythm analysis that shows "vibe" as a mathematically objective object in information space. feeds conduct people on a pre-cognitive level. institutions generate a rhythm in digital space that keeps a community oriented toward their signal — it's happening below conscious awareness.

julian on the 4chan source code as protocol weapon: he made a poster of the entire source code of 4chan (futaba.php) — under a thousand lines of code. written by an anonymous guy in japan in 2001, translated to english a few years later to start 4chan. the downstream social effects (every meme for 15 years, alt-right movements, incel movements) had "very similar analogous movements earlier in japan emerging from futaba.php." same code, same social physics, every country.

approaching a thesis statement: "if you change the rules, you change the subjectivity." the 4chan source code is the perfect example — deploy a few hundred lines of code and the downstream social effects are predictable and similar everywhere.

## key points

- vibeocracy (jacqueline fent): we live in a vibeocracy where community personality and vibe-conducting are the critical skills.
- vibe is not just aesthetic — informational-rhythm analysis shows it as a mathematically objective object in information space. feeds conduct people pre-cognitively.
- 4chan source code (futaba.php): under 1000 lines of code. deployed in japan, translated to english, produced analogous social movements in every country. a protocol weapon.
- thesis: "if you change the rules, you change the subjectivity." the protocol layer determines the social output.
- para-institutions maintain community through rhythm and vibe in digital space, not just through content or courses.
