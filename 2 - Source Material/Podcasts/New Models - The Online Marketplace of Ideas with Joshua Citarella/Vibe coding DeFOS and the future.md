## transcription

josh on the current state of social media and music: everyone hates it. music marketing firms have become "the equivalent of whatever that Russian state troll farm is" — fake accounts, networks of hundreds of meme pages, pushing content to 10 million people. technically legal, against TOS. artists don't want to post anymore and pay premiums to not touch social media.

julian: with vibe coding, it becomes feasible to design a good social media platform for a niche interest — finding that "perfect in-between space of the right number of people where it feels special." also: you can design your web experience yourself.

julian's conspiracy theory: tech oligarchs' ideology is that everything will fragment into autonomous city states. with zero-knowledge proofs (anonymous and verified simultaneously), someone can vibe-code an open-source uber that replaces uber. the oligarchs know their monopoly on middleman infrastructure is time-limited. "i wonder if the tech oligarchs actually project out and say our time of monopolizing this middleman space is limited and we need to get a trillion dollars so that we can escape to new zealand before all of this collapses."

josh agrees: web2 platforms that started as lean competitors have become rent extractors, drains on the economy. "profit slop — profit without production."

julian on DeFOS (dark forest operating system): being built by yancey. a one-stop shop for communities — all the functions (publishing, patreon, substack, instagram) built into one thing. critically: you can build in micropayments and remuneration for contributors. "a personal cul-de-sac" rather than something that operates at state capacity.

josh: at DNR's scale (400-500 published contributors), keeping track of membership and contributions becomes really important. DeFOS as a "net positive for society."

the unbundling will continue: julian expects a total proliferation of social apps. the innovation will be in "roads that connect these different apps and communities in ways that feel healthy."

## key points

- music marketing has become indistinguishable from state troll farms — fake accounts, meme networks, paid push to millions. artists pay premiums to not touch social media.
- vibe coding makes it feasible to build niche social platforms and open-source alternatives to platform middlemen (open-source uber).
- julian's conspiracy theory: tech oligarchs know their middleman monopoly is time-limited (zero-knowledge proofs, open source alternatives). accumulating capital to escape before collapse.
- web2 platforms became rent extractors: "profit slop — profit without production."
- DeFOS (dark forest operating system): one-stop shop for communities with built-in micropayments for contributors. the personal cul-de-sac vs. state-capacity platform.
- future: total proliferation of social apps. innovation in the connections between them, not the apps themselves.
