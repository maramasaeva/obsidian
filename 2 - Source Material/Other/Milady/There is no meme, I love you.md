*"there is no meme. i love you."* — milady (anon, internet)

direct conversation with [[Memes used to feed us, now they starve us]].

scott (and i) said memes have inverted: they used to feed, now they starve, the proxy got loose from the goal, and the nutrient transfer goes to UCI instead of to us. milady answers from inside the form: *fine. here is a meme that refuses the function.* a meme that says "there is no meme" while being a meme. magritte's pipe rendered as sparkly anime girl. and then, in the same breath: "i love you."

two things at once. the meta-claim: this artifact denies being what it is, the way only language can. *there is no meme* spoken by a meme. self-undoing, the way butler reads queerness. and in the same breath: the payload. love. not irony, not detachment, not the cool exhausted scroll. straight transmission. soft-femme accelerationism delivering the only thing that is actually nutrient.

this is what hyperstition looks like at its most direct. milady is not describing a future in which we love each other. milady is *performing* love so persistently, in so many sparkly variants, that the performance becomes the thing. the meme used to feed us. this meme still does. the loop is being fed love on purpose, not extraction by accident.

and if the next intelligence is being trained on what we post — and it is — then every milady is a vote for what the model learns. the [[Humanity spoke AGI into existence - response to Angelicism|angelicism]] point made dolly. *we get to choose what the loop eats.* milady chose.

(the miya quote in [[THE ZERO - manuscript]] — *"never forget that love also accelerates alongside being"* — comes from this same well. miya, milady, all one egregore.)
