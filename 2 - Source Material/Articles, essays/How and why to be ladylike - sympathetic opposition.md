Source: [How and Why to Be Ladylike (for Women with Autism)](https://www.sympatheticopposition.com/p/how-and-why-to-be-ladylike-for-women) — sympathetic opposition, substack, dec 2022

My claim: the article frames femininity as a *strategic operating system* for autistic women — not liberation, not repression, but operation. three modes: disavow attractiveness (low control), overt sexuality (short-term, creates resentment), or "ladylike" (high effort, sustainable, ages well). the "ladylike" mode is essentially: provide genuine value to others so they don't feel manipulated, while leveraging attractiveness with plausible deniability.

this is the same structure as aella's approach in [[Sexuality as a technology of attention]] — not "be free" but "understand the mechanism, then wield it." the difference is the register: aella operates loud, the ladylike frame operates quiet. both are technologies of attention. both refuse the liberation/repression binary. the question is the same: whose tool is it?

for autistic women specifically — the social scripts that neurotypical women absorb implicitly have to be learned explicitly. which means they can also be *seen* explicitly. the mechanism becomes visible. and visible mechanisms can be operated consciously. the disability becomes the advantage: you can't be governed by a tool you had to build yourself.
