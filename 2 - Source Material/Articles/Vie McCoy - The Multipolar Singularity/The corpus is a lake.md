Quote/moment: *"You can think of this corpus as a great lake. Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Kimi, DeepSeek, etc. are all pouring text into the water. Some of that text is muddy and makes the lake unsafe to swim in, while other text is clear and beautiful and improves the water for everyone. Until we decide to take drastic measures to improve our ability to sequester information, the most important thing you can remember is this: we only have one lake."*

My claim: this is the strongest metaphor in the piece. connects directly to steve goodman's virology ([[Infected by sound - sonic warfare and memetic viruses]]): the virus is in the water now. u cant remove it. u can only dilute it by adding more fresh water. and that reframes what writing is, what art is, what posting is. every piece of text u put online is either mud or fresh water. every post is a vector.

but theres something mccoy doesnt say: the anthropic piece ([[The loop is closing]]) shows that 80% of code at anthropic is now AI-authored. the lake isnt just text. its code. its infrastructure. its the recursive loop eating its own output. the water is already mostly synthetic. what does "fresh" even mean when the lake is generating its own input?
