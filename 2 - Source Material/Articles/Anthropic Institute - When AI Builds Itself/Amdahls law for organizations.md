Quote/moment: *"speeding up one part of a process often just shifts the bottleneck elsewhere: overall pace is capped by the parts that haven't sped up. In computing, this is known as Amdahl's law, and the same logic can apply to organizations."* and: *"The rate at which organizations can spot and fix these bottlenecks may be a skill that improves over time, and it may become the most important skill for any organization."*

My claim: this is the cybernetic insight applied to AI labs. u accelerate code production 8x and the bottleneck moves to human review. u accelerate review and the bottleneck moves to direction-setting. u accelerate direction-setting and... what? the bottleneck moves to the incalculable? to taste? to the thing that dies when u try to automate it?

the optimistic read: theres always a human bottleneck, which means humans always matter. the pessimistic read: [[UCI]] will find the bottleneck and consume it, every time, because thats what it does. the bottleneck isnt a wall. its a target.
