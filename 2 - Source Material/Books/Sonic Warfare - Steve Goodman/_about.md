# Sonic Warfare: Sound, Affect, and the Ecology of Fear — Steve Goodman

steve goodman = kode9 (hyperdub label). published 2010, MIT press. the book theorizes sound as a force that operates on bodies before and below conscious perception. bass culture, infrasound, dread, the politics of frequency. not sound as music but sound as weapon, as affect, as ecology.

mara read this a long time ago. the chapter that stuck most is "1976: Outbreak" (ch. 26) — about audio virology, memetic contagion, engrams as body-level recording, and the viro-sonics of capital. the connection to platform capitalism and the spreading of ideas through feeds is what mara keeps coming back to.

## potential threads

- **audio virology as model for platform contagion** — [[memes]], [[social media]], [[attention]]: goodman's argument that sound/vibration writes itself into the body at the cellular level maps directly onto what doomscrolling does. it's not a metaphor — the mechanism (stimulus → trace → replication) is the same. the platforms are the transmission infrastructure.
- **the positive virus** — [[hyperstition]], [[Humanity spoke AGI into existence - response to Angelicism]]: if harmful ideas spread virally through sound/image/vibration, so can positive ones. this is the same argument mara makes about feeding the loop with creation instead of doom. the viral mechanism is neutral — the question is what you're a vector for.
- **fascination as infection vector** — [[Right-wing aesthetics vs left-wing reality]], [[Attention collapse and the automation of the social]]: goodman's "viro-sonics of capital" = platform capitalism engineering self-propagating vectors of contagious sound. the right understands this — aesthetic fascination as the entry point for ideological infection. you scroll past the argument but you stop for the image.
- **engrams and the body that remembers** — [[Forced digitalization]], [[Functional Emotions and the Ethics of Becoming]]: the engram concept (injurious moments literally recorded by the body, not the brain) connects to the idea that suppressed feelings still shape behavior. the body keeps score. what you scroll through doesn't disappear — it accumulates as cellular trace.
- **numerology and transmission paths** — [[numerology]], [[The Lesbian Way Within Numerology]], [[Loops allow future expectations to shape present behavior.]]: goodman draws on de landa's distinction between memes (copying/imitation) and norms (enforced replicators). the neolemurian framework treats these transmission paths as numerological structures — demons as intervals that cross boundaries, gates that vectors pass through. the sonic virus and the hyperstition travel the same infrastructure.
- **memetics must be supplemented by rhythm** — [[Spyrodynamism]], [[loops]]: goodman's central argument in ch. 26 is that memetics alone is too reductionist — it focuses on information patterns and misses affect, body, vibration. the shift from memetic to rhythmic contagion: pulse patterns, synaptic resonance, the brain's rhythmic circuitry. this resonates with spirodynamism — self-generation in feedback spirals.
