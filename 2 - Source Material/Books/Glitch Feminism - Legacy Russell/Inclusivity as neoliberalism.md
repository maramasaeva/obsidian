*"Facebook's fifty-eight gender options (and three pronouns, lest we forget!), first made available for users in 2014, was not a radical gesture — it was neoliberalism at its finest. If a body without a name is an error, providing more names, while proffering inclusivity, does not resolve the issue of the binary body. Rather, it makes and requires a box to be ticked, a categorization to be determined."* (p. 121, ch. 10, Glitch Mobilizes)

adding more boxes to a broken system is still the same logic of classification and control. the question for AI governance is not "how do we add more categories" but "why does the system require categorization at all." russell links this to galloway's "generic difference" — the radical move is to abstain entirely from the biopolitical regime of tagging and sorting bodies. keep all boxes unticked. a body emancipated from ever being asked to register its traces online.

this is the sharpest thing to say on a "trustworthy AI" panel: your inclusivity framework is still a framework of control. you cannot fix the house by adding rooms if the foundation is classification.
