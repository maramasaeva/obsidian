Quote/moment: *"Striving to learn, to study, to analyze and calculate — those are things to be learned, yes, but that learning... moves you away from wisdom. The Dao within seeks to 'enlighten' others; wisdom comes from emptying. This idea almost invites misunderstanding and manipulation — again, words misled."* (p. 110)

and confucius, after studying the ancient books for a long time, goes to see laozi. laozi says: *"crows and magpies hatch from eggs in nests. young fish come out of freshly bubbled water. bees and wasps emerge from any of nothing besides. and for us, even as we live through it, for so long we've not paid attention to the flow of ceaseless change in everything around me. but unless I become one with ceaseless change, how can I seek to change anyone?"* (p. 111)

My claim: the opposite of the accumulation model. you don't prepare for plzdontkillus by cramming every mentor's bibliography. you prepare by emptying enough to actually see the people when you meet them. the confucius trap: i've studied all the books, i can recite everything, why don't i understand? because understanding isn't in the accumulation. it's in becoming one with the change.
