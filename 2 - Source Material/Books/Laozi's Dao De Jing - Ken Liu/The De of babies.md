Quote/moment: *"Someone who holds the power of De is like a newborn baby. Venomous insects don't sting them; fierce beasts don't seize them; swooping raptors don't claw them. Their bones and sinews are weak, yet their grasp is strong. They know not the union of male and female, yet their organ is erect. They cry all day without their voice cracking. They're at the peak of harmony."* (ch. 55, p. 119)

and ken liu on this: trolling section compares this to the twitter-communication problem — you cry all day without your voice cracking, for they're at the peak of harmony. the baby doesn't modulate, doesn't strategize, doesn't perform. the baby just *is*, and that is De.

My claim: the newborn baby as the model for powerful communication. not strategy, not optimization, but a kind of undefended vitality that predators can't touch because there's nothing to grab onto. no ego-surface to attack. relevant to the question of how to be online without being consumed by it.
