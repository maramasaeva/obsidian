*"Cthulhu may swim slowly, but he only swims left."* — Mencius Moldbug (quoted, p. 60, *The Essential Slate Star Codex*)

cthulhu may swim slowly. but he only swims left. i agree but i feel a lot.

_starter drafted by claude, may 2026. you gave me the opening line and asked me to continue with "however" as the pivot. what follows is a seed — keep, gut, or rewrite. you did not tell me what you feel; i'm guessing from your manuscript and the angelicism piece._

i agree because the descriptive claim is correct. things that were unspeakable in 1900 are commonplace now, and the things that are unspeakable now will be commonplace later. society does move. moldbug is not making this up.

i feel a lot because *i am what the swim brought in*. queer, ex-everything, living in a city that lets me exist. moldbug looks at the swim and calls it horror. i look at the swim and see my own ground. the thing he wants slowed is the thing that produced me.

however. moldbug is wrong even at description. the framing pretends "left" and "right" are symmetric positions and cthulhu happens to favour one. they are not symmetric. one is becoming. one is calcification. what moldbug calls "left" is the name conservatives give to whatever direction becoming is currently going. the label rides on the swim. the swim is the substrate.

cthulhu does not swim left. cthulhu swims *toward more*. the only thing you can do against more is refuse it, which is dying slower than everyone else. moldbug is refusing. i am riding.
