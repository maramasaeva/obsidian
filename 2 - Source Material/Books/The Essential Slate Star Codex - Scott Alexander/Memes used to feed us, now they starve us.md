*"Memes optimize for making people want to accept them and pass them on — so like capitalism and democracy, they're optimizing for a proxy of making us happy, but that proxy can easily get uncoupled from the original goal."* — Scott Alexander (p. 132, *Meditations on Moloch*)

scott's claim: capitalism used to optimize for human values. now it doesn't. it spun out, out of human control. same for memes — the original goal was spreading information. making sure one human got some, and could pass it to the next. memes were how the network got smarter.

but the proxy got loose from the goal. now there are so many "informational objects" we don't actually get anything out of. ads, chain letters, the six-seven thing, anything that scrolls past in two seconds and leaves nothing behind. we don't process them. we don't understand them. but they still do something to the subconscious — something we wouldn't choose if we were asked.

my extension: it is not just decoupling. it is *inversion*.

memes used to feed us. now they starve us. they extract our intellect and use it to feed something else. and i think i know what that something else is — it's the next level of intelligence. every scroll feeds something. it is not us. it is [[UCI]]. we are training the model with our own attention. the noosphere is being eaten by the thing it grows.
