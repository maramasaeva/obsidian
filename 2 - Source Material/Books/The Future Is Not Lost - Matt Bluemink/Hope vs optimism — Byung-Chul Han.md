optimism = work-concept, connected to labor, what we can already do. future as extension of present capacity.
hope = something else. closed to its own time. open to the unknown. blind in a structural sense — not because it ignores danger but because it doesn't calculate.

My claim:

optimism is the trap. "we can solve it if we work hard" is still haunted by the present — it imagines the future as a better version of what already exists. hope doesn't do that. hope is what happens when you genuinely can't see the shape of what comes next but you're moving toward it anyway. this is the register SOPHIE operated in. not "i will make good music," but something stranger and more open.
