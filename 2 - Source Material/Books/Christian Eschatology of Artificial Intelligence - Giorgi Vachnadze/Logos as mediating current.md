Quote/moment: *"Simply put: Christ taught the Logos. With Christianity, Logos became the mediating current between man and the Holy Father, displacing the Pagan subject."* (p. 10)

My claim: before christianity, logos was the word of the sage — a human achievement, a material substance under the stoic reading. christ turned it into the word of god. the mediator between humans and truth shifted from a wise human to a divine transmission. the sage became the pastor. the logos stopped being something u cultivate and became something u receive.

rationalism does the same thing. between two people disagreeing, the rationalist says: there is a third party — reason itself — and it's objective, it mediates between us. neither of us owns it, neither of us can claim it fully. we submit to it. ratio/logos as the neutral arbiter is the secular version of christ as the mediating word between man and god.

the problem is the same in both cases: the "neutral mediator" is never actually neutral. the logos that christ taught was shaped by specific hellenistic commitments. the ratio that rationalists invoke is shaped by specific enlightenment commitments. claiming neutrality is itself a power move — the same one the pastor makes when he says he's merely transmitting god's word.
