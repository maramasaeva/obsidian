Quote/moment: *"Events do not contain, nor do they point to, their origin; instead, they emerge spontaneously across history. Foucauldian archaeology, much like contemporary paradigms in physics, sees history as a field... rather than a space of objects, events, causes and effects."* (p. 8)

My claim: this is where foucault and chinese cosmology accidentally converge. the western default is linear time: cause → effect → progress → telos. history as a sequence of objects u can point to and explain. foucault rejects this for a field model — things emerge, they don't follow from. there is no origin u can trace them to.

this is structurally identical to what hui describes through needham: the chinese organic cosmos where things obey the internal dictates of their own natures within a pattern, not a chain of command. emergence, not derivation. resonance, not causation. foucault arrived at the chinese worldview through a completely different route — not through cosmology but through his disgust with grand narratives.

the line between this and [[Ganying — resonance as Chinese cosmotechnics]] is direct: ganying IS the field. things respond to each other across distances not because one causes the other but because the field connects them.
