Quote/moment: *"Needless to say, the practice, control and surveillance of sexuality played an important role. Needless to say, transgressions of the sexual sort were only one of many different ways of acting in a sinful manner."* (p. 7)

My claim: foucault's point through vachnadze: sexuality is not the essence of identity, it's a technology of governance. the church didn't repress sexuality because sex is inherently sinful — it used sexuality as a strategic entry point for governing subjects. the flesh is the master-signifier of christian power. but sexuality is contingent, not fundamental. it's one of many sinful acts, just the most useful one for producing docile subjects.

this matters because the same technique reappears in algorithmic governance. the platform doesn't care about ur sexuality per se — it cares about it as a vector for engagement, confession, self-disclosure. the content changes, the technique stays. sexuality becomes data, data becomes the new flesh.
