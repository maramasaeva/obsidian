# Christian Eschatology of Artificial Intelligence — Giorgi Vachnadze

**Author:** Giorgi Vachnadze
**Published:** 2024 (Becoming Press, Berlin/Nicosia)
**Shelf:** currently-reading
**Started:** 2026-06-16

---

vachnadze's central move: take two discursive formations that claim to be completely external to one another — christian-pastoral techniques of self-formation and technocratic-neoliberal technologies of the self — and show that they draw on the same forms of subjugation, produce the same types of subjects, generate the same docility. the book is not saying christianity caused AI or AI is secretly christian. it's saying both regimes of truth share a deep structure that neither wants to acknowledge.

heavily foucauldian. draws on the *confessions of the flesh* (foucault's 4th volume on history of sexuality), dreyfus's AI critique, stypinska on digital pastorate, carrière on thinking-as-dwelling, koopman on datafied personhood. the method is genealogical — not what AI *is*, but what historical conditions of power made it *thinkable*.

dedicated to achim szepanski.

## potential threads

- **algocracy as eschatology** — [[eschatology]], [[cosmotechnics]]: vachnadze asks why secular AI discourse sounds increasingly like religious prophecy. hui's cosmotechnics framework would say: because it IS a cosmotechnics — a specific relation between technics and cosmos disguised as universal reason. the eschatological structure is not a metaphor, it's the actual cosmological frame operating underneath.
- **pastoral power went digital** — [[surveillance]], [[social media]], [[exocapitalism]]: the confessional didn't disappear, it dispersed into platforms. stypinska's "spectral subjects" are the new confessing animals. the algorithm is the new pastor. connects directly to mara's think workshop concerns about attention and self-formation.
- **self-negation across traditions** — [[tao]], [[cosmotechnics]]: the christian demand to negate the self and hand it to god resonates strangely with mou zongsan's *self-negation of liangzhi* — but in reverse. mou says the moral mind dims itself voluntarily so the analytical can develop. christianity says the sinful self must be destroyed so the divine element can surface. same structure, opposite valence.
- **the three assumptions as cosmological commitments** — [[epistemology]], [[cybernetics]], [[zeroes and ones]]: binary reduction, phenomenological reduction, formalizability. these aren't scientific findings — they're cosmological axioms. the faith in computation is a faith in a specific cosmos.
- **desire loops** — [[desire]], [[loops]], [[exocapitalism]]: we desire the desire of the other, and capitalism simulates the object of satisfaction. AI is positioned as the ultimate satisfaction-object. the loop of simulated desire is the loop of capital.
- **value alignment as original sin** — [[value alignment]], [[ai safety]]: the claim that AI can't govern itself because it's inherently misaligned mirrors the christian claim that the self can't govern itself because it's inherently fallen. who aligns whom? an ape wouldn't want to align a human.
