the bayesian thing is interesting — the "possibility of readjustment" is essentially a bayesian stance. not a fixed program, not a manifesto. u work from tactics to strategy, u remain open to updating. foucault's micro-level method IS a form of bayesian reasoning about power: observe the evidence, update ur model, don't commit to grand narratives that can't be revised.
