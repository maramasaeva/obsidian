Quote/moment: *"the persistence of 'capitalism as a religion'"* (p. 25, via stypinska)

*"AI driven discourse operates in a way quite similar to the Christian discourse on God."* (p. 25)

My claim: capitalism as a religion is not a metaphor. it's a structural claim. capitalism persists the same way a religion persists — through techniques of self-formation, through discursive regimes that produce subjects who reproduce the system, through a transcendent object (growth, progress, the market) that functions exactly like god: always beyond, always demanding, always promising.

AI slots into this as the new god-object. it's the thing that will finally solve all problems. it's the ultimate consumer good AND the ultimate producer. and the discourse around it — the breathless prophesying, the fear of misalignment, the eschatological urgency — operates exactly like christian discourse on god. not metaphorically. structurally.

what's interesting is the loop quality. capitalism as religion produces the conditions for AI-as-god, which reinforces capitalism-as-religion. the loop is self-sustaining. u don't need a conspiracy. u just need a cosmotechnics that was built for this — a cosmos where a transcendent lawgiver is the natural shape of power.
