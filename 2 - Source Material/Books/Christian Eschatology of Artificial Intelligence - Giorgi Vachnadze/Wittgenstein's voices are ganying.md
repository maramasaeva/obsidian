Quote/moment: *"The more you listen the more you will hear, and voices that would have been hardly audible at first will then speak more and more distinctly and new ones will turn up."* (wittgenstein, via guter, 2024 — quoted in vachnadze p. 86)

My claim: wittgenstein's analysis of musical understanding — the voices from within that emerge when you listen, that multiply the more attention you give them, that can never be exhausted or finalised — is structurally identical to ganying (感應, feeling-response). the confucians say the resonance between heaven and human is "as objective and concrete as the resonance of two musical instruments" ([[Ganying — resonance as Chinese cosmotechnics]]). wittgenstein says: the only way to understand a piece of music is to play it and notice how little you understand, then listen to the voices inside you, then play again, and each time new voices emerge.

same structure: a coupling between the human and something that exceeds the human (the cosmos, the composition), mediated by the act of playing (technics), producing an understanding that cannot be externalised, spatialised, or computed.

the quantified self can measure your heart rate while you play. it can track how many hours you practiced. it can compare your tempo against a reference recording. none of this captures the voices. the mobile confessional replaces ganying with data — replaces resonance with metrics. and [[Music is cosmotechnics — grimes, ganying, and the last musician]] already asks: if AI "plays" music, does it channel something or does it simulate channeling? vachnadze gives the wittgensteinian answer: a machine can reproduce the score, but reproduction without understanding is precisely what laziness looks like. the laziness is not physical — it is affective. the refusal to listen.

and vachnadze's point about NLP/chatGPT as *the automated confessional par excellence* lands differently through this lens. the pastoral confessional was already a degraded form of listening — the pastor hears your sins, but the point is direction, not resonance. the algorithm doesn't even hear. it processes. there is no ganying with a dataset.
