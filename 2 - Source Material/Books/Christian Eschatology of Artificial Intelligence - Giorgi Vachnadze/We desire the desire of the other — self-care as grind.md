My claim: we desire desire. not the thing, but the wanting of the thing. and we simulate us desiring desire. the whole loop is simulation: the object of satisfaction is simulated, the desire for it is simulated, the self that desires is simulated. it's lacanian to the core but vachnadze maps it onto the platform economy and it lands perfectly.

under the current regime AI is positioned as the ultimate object of satisfaction — the thing that will finally solve everything. but the satisfaction is always deferred (like god's kingdom). and the desire is manufactured by the same apparatus that promises to fulfill it. we simulate the problems, we simulate the solutions.

the self-care → grind flip is the most visible version. "love urself" on social media means: produce ur self-love as content. document ur healing. monetise ur growth. the moment self-care stops being productive, it becomes wasteful, and the disciplinary apparatus kicks in. discipline over motivation. the gamified apparatus reverts to punishment the second u stop performing.

it's not actually self-love. it's the simulation of self-love in service of the same production function that the christian confessional served: the production of a docile, self-disclosing subject who willingly hands herself over to the apparatus.
