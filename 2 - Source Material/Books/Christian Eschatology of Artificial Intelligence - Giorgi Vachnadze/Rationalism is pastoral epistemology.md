Quote/moment: *"Turing machines, Wittgenstein says, 'are really humans who calculate.' … machines are humans who (only) calculate, that is, without thinking."* (p. 94) and: *"for the last 60 to 70 years or so, basic linear algebra has been re-branded and marketed as human (artificial) intelligence."* (p. 95)

My claim: the rationalist community is built on the assumption that thinking IS calculating. that reasoning can be formalized into bayesian updates, logical sequences, expected value calculations. the sequences, lesswrong, the entire epistemic culture — it's a framework that says: if u can't formalize ur argument, it's not a real argument. if u can't put a probability on it, u don't really believe it.

but that IS the cosmological assumption vachnadze traces from christianity through computation. god created the universe according to mathematical laws → nature is formalizable → the human mind is formalizable → u can translate it into an artificial mind. the rationalists inherited this whole chain. they just dropped the word "god" and kept the structure. what they call "rationality" is a local cosmotechnics ([[cosmotechnics]]) — one specific cosmology's way of knowing — wearing universal clothes. [[rationalism]] is what hui would call the western cosmotechnics of reasoning itself ([[Cosmotechnics — technics under cosmological conditions]]).

wittgenstein's distinction between thinking and calculating is exactly what the rationalist community cannot see. calculating = following a set of rules and getting an answer. thinking = something that emerges from consciousness, from embodiment, from the normative social practices of a lifeworld ([[The theologico-machinic unconscious is western cosmotechnics]]). u can formalize the calculation. u can't formalize the thinking. but if ur whole epistemology assumes thinking IS calculation, then the distinction doesn't exist for u. and that's exactly the "bewitchment" wittgenstein warns about — being held captive by a picture.

so yudkowsky wanting intelligence to be LEGIBLE ([[Yudkowsky — what i actually need to know before pdku]]) is the same desire as the pastoral demand for total transparency of the soul. confess everything. make the inner life surveyable. the sequences are a confessional practice — formalize ur beliefs, update them publicly, submit to peer correction. it's exagoreusis for nerds.
