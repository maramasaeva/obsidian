Quote/moment: *"We want to trace the complex processes whereby the concepts of God, AI and Capital operate in unison to produce the Confessional, Digital and Entrepreneurial Subject, through multiple Pastoral and Bio-Economic Technologies of the Self."* (p. 78)

My claim: vachnadze describes three subjects — the christian confessional subject, the homo oeconomicus, and the cybernetic quantified self — converging into a single hybrid formation. this is [[mode collapse]] applied to subjectivity. the digital pastoral doesn't offer more options. it collapses every form of self-relation into one metric framework: the algorithmic dispositif.

the structure is exactly what hui describes in [[Cosmotechnics — technics under cosmological conditions]]: the universality assumption = mode collapse applied to civilisational thinking. when you assume there's only one kind of technics, you've already collapsed. when you assume there's only one kind of subject — the subject who tracks, confesses, optimises — you've already collapsed the subject.

the three regimes vachnadze traces (greek aphrodisia → christian flesh → cybernetic self-quantification) look like a history of increasing mode collapse. the aphrodisia still allowed for multiple styles of self-mastery. the diagram of the flesh narrowed it to one axis (purity/sin). the quantified self narrows it further to data points. each iteration is more total, more unavoidable, less capable of accommodating heterogeneous forms of life. the cybernetic subject is what remains when every mode of becoming has been collapsed into a single output: the optimised, productive, trackable self.

vachnadze himself doesn't use the term mode collapse, but his description of bergsonian duration as resistance — "an internal multiplicity of heterogeneity, of qualitative discrimination, of difference in kind" (deleuze, via vachnadze p. 85) — is the exact opposite of mode collapse. duration IS what mode collapse destroys: the heterogeneity of experience. the incomputable richness of a subject who cannot be reduced to a data point.
