Quote/moment: *"AI discourse seeks to achieve several things: First, it aims to reduce thought to a techne and second to uncover the biological system as a kind of technology that would be able to manifest thinking."* (p. 20)

*"the Christian injunction to obey is mirrored in the neoliberal imperative to improve, both in effect seeking (and to large and unfortunate extent; successfully managing) to deploy a biopolitical regime of docility."* (p. 21)

My claim: the structural parallel is clean. christianity: obey god → salvation. neoliberalism: improve urself → success. both produce docile subjects. both frame submission as freedom. the christian gives herself over to a sacred technology (the confessional, the pastoral, baptism). the neoliberal gives herself over to a secular technology (the app, the coaching program, the self-improvement regimen). in both cases: *one gives oneself over to a techno-discursive apparatus, ultimately (allegedly) to an AI or — to God.*

the thought-to-techne reduction is the enabling move. if thought IS calculation, then thought CAN be optimised. if thought can be optimised, then there's an imperative to optimise it. the is slides into the ought. u go from "the brain is a computer" to "u should upgrade ur software" in one step. and suddenly improving urself is not a choice but a duty. the neoliberal improve is as compulsory as the christian obey.
