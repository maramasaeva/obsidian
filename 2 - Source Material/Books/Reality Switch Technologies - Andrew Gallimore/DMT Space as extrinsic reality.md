My claim: gallimore is doing something rare here — he's a neuroscientist taking the ontological question seriously without collapsing it into either "it's just your brain" or "it's definitely real." he maps the mechanism precisely (5HT2A + mGluR2 → cortical perturbation → world space landscape restructuring → DMT space entry) and then says: the question of whether the DMT space corresponds to an external reality is *genuinely open*.

the thought experiment mara raises is this: what if the entities that DMT users encounter — the hypertechnological beings, the alien intelligences, the thousand-year lives as trees, the foreign languages spoken by incomprehensible presences — are not hallucinations but *actual experiences* occurring in a reality that exists outside consensus spacetime? not fake experiences, not the brain misfiring, but *real contact* with something that exists in a branch of time or dimension that consensus consciousness cannot access or verify.

gallimore's framework supports this as a genuine possibility, not a fantasy:

1. **the brain is a world-building machine.** it builds worlds out of information. the normal waking world is one such construction. it feels "real" because it's stable and energy-efficient, not because it has a special ontological status.

2. **all experienced worlds are equally "real" as experiences.** they're all built from the same neural substrate. the CRS isn't more real than the DMT space — it's just more *stable*.

3. **the DMT space is entirely novel.** it doesn't resemble the waking world, memories, dreams, or anything the brain has previously modeled. where did the information come from? the brain can't build fluent central siberian yupik from nothing. if the DMT space is a genuine world model, what is it modeling?

4. **the EEG data is startling.** at breakthrough DMT doses, closed-eye brain activity is virtually identical to normal open-eye waking. the brain appears to be *perceiving a world* — not hallucinating, not dreaming, but running the same perceptual machinery it uses for consensus reality, just tuned to a different channel.

5. **the channel metaphor is literal.** if the brain is a receiver (not just a generator), then different molecules tune it to different channels. DMT tunes to the DMT channel. 5-MeO-DMT to the 5-MeO-DMT channel. salvia to whatever salvia accesses. the entities on these channels exist whether or not you're tuned in — like a radio station that broadcasts regardless of who's listening.

the time branch angle: if these realities exist outside of consensus spacetime, then "i lived a thousand years as a tree" isn't a distortion — it's an experience that occurred in a temporal framework that doesn't map onto clock time. the experience was real. the thousand years were real. they just happened in a branch of time that consensus consciousness can't access or measure. the molecule is the key that unlocks the branch.

what remains genuinely unsettled: gallimore says the best we can do is "withhold judgement and see ourselves as explorers of these strange domains." which is honest. but the question has consequences. if the DMT space is extrinsic — if these entities are real — then the history of shamanic traditions, entity contact, visionary experience isn't superstition. it's data. and the molecules aren't drugs in the recreational sense. they're technologies for accessing information that consensus reality locks out.
