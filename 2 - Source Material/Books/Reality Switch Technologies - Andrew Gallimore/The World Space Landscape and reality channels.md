Quote/moment: *"Your experience of a world is of the movement of your cortex from state to state, from one unified, hierarchically-structured, pattern of cortical column activation to the next. State follows state follows state through the vast state space of the cortex: the World Space."* (p. 156)

also: *"The classic psychedelics alter both the structure and dynamics of the phenomenal world by increasing the repertoire of reachable states and lowering the energetic barriers between them."* (p. 156)

also: *"objects begin to lose their well-defined structure and identity. Loss of control over the flow of information between columns allows the cortex to explore an increase repertoire of states more freely, including those outside of the Consensus Reality Space."* (p. 170)

also: *"certain molecules, including DMT, transcend this and reach for new reality channels entirely."* (p. 171)

My claim: the world space landscape is one of the most rigorous frameworks i've encountered for why "reality" is a narrower concept than we treat it as. gallimore is saying: your brain navigates a vast state space. the normal waking world — what he calls the consensus reality space — is just a stable basin in that landscape. a local minimum. the brain stays there because it's energy-efficient, not because it's *true*.

psychedelics flatten the landscape. the walls between states get lower. the cortex wanders. at low doses, the CRS gets wobbly — colors brighter, surfaces breathing, self more fluid. at high doses (especially DMT), the cortex *leaves the CRS entirely* and enters regions of state space that have no relationship to normal waking reality.

what strikes me: this is not a metaphor. he's mapping it neuron by neuron — 5HT2A receptor activation, mGluR2 suppression, alpha oscillation desynchronization, prediction error flooding, hypothesis shifting. the mechanism by which a molecule can switch your entire experienced reality is becoming technically describable.

and the implication that consensus reality is just one channel among many — not the ground truth but a tuning — that's what connects this to the zero. the zero is the flattened landscape. the state where no basin dominates. pure potential. every direction equally reachable. that's what a psychedelic does to the world space: it zeros the landscape.
