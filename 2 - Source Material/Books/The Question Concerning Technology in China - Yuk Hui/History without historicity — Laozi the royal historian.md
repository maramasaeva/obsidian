Quote/moment: on Wang Fuzhi via Julien — *"the tradition in [which Wang] inscribed his thinking was never affected by a theophanic reading of history."* Wang "can hardly speak of the progress of history when he does not oppose nature to history." (§20.3, p. 261)

and the paradox of Laozi: *"it is surprising to note that Laozi, the author of Dao De Ching, was a historian of the Zhou dynasty; or more precisely, that he was the official historian of the royal library."* — yet the book's first line is *"Dao called dao is not dao. Names can name no lasting name."* Hui asks: *"Should we read this as a refusal of the writing of history, provided that what he means by history is something that always escapes and is continually changing?"* (p. 262)

My claim: china has a history; what it lacks is *historicity* — the discourse that treats time as a line you progress along, redeemed at the end. and the tell is gorgeous: the man who wrote the most time-refusing book in the canon was the *royal librarian*, the keeper of the official record. he had all of history in his hands and what he wrote was *the dao that can be named is not the eternal dao.*

because in laozi's time the historian's job wasn't to build historical consciousness — it was to read the ancient texts for *advice on governance.* history as a working tool for the present, not a line pointing at a future. the political use of the past took precedence over any story of progress. no opposition of nature to history (Wang Fuzhi), so no progress *through* history, so no theophany — no god revealing himself across time, no arc that bends toward anything. just pattern, return, resonance.

this is the deep root of [[Technological consciousness as consciousness of time]]: if your cosmology never sets nature against history, you never get the finitude-anxiety that makes you build prostheses to *beat time.* you don't archive against death; you resonate with the season. different time → different reason to build → different technics.

and it doubles back onto the writing: a librarian who refuses to fix the name is doing what the open loop does — keeping the system from closing into a told story. *the past never truly happened* in the mythomancy sense isn't sloppiness, it's a cosmotechnical stance. history that always escapes is history that stays editable. that's where the sorcery lives.
