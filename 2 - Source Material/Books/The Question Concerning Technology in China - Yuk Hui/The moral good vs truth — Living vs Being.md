Quote/moment: *"unlike the Chinese concept of technics as realising the 'moral good' of the cosmos, Heidegger's interpretation of Aristotle's technics reveals 'truth' (aletheia), the unconcealment of Being."* (p. 62)

*"Mou Zongsan distinguishes Chinese moral metaphysics from a metaphysics of morals, while for Mou, metaphysics is only possible on the basis of the moral."* (p. 63)

*"Compared to Presocratic and classical Greek philosophy, during the same period of history in China, neither the question of Being nor the question of techné comprised the core questions of philosophy. Rather than 'Being', what was common to Confucian and Daoist teaching was the question of 'Living', in the sense of leading a moral or good life."* (p. 63)

My claim: this is the deepest split in the book. western philosophy asks: what is true? (being, truth, aletheia, unconcealment). chinese philosophy asks: what is good? (the moral, living well, resonance with heaven). these aren't opposite questions — good and true are not opposites. they just don't refer to the same thing. truth here is more about what is scientifically, ontologically revealed. moral good is about what is spiritually, morally right — something that transcends correctness. and crucially: what heidegger means by truth is not scientific truth either. it's not logical proof. it's the relation between dasein and its world — how being-there reveals what things mean. but even heidegger's expanded truth is still *truth*, still unconcealment, still oriented toward what-is. the chinese question is oriented toward how-to-live.

if your philosophy is organized around truth and being, your technics will be oriented toward revelation, measurement, extraction — bringing what is hidden into the light. this is the promethean structure. greek techné reveals truth through violence ([[Techné as violence — Heidegger on the Greek relation to making]]).

if your philosophy is organized around the moral good and living, your technics will be oriented toward harmony, resonance, balance — maintaining the relation between human action and cosmic order. this is the cosmotechnical structure. chinese technics doesn't reveal truth — it enacts the good.

this reframes AI alignment entirely. alignment research asks: how do we make AI's values true to ours? this is a truth-question, a being-question. cosmotechnics would ask: how do we make AI's activity good — not just accurate but morally resonant with the order it participates in? "aligned" is a truth-word. "harmonious" is a moral-good-word. we're building alignment when maybe we should be building harmony.
