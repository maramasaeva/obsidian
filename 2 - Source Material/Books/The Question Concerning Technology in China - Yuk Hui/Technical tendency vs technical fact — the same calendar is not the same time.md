Quote/moment: *"technical apparatuses, as extensions of organs — and, as prostheses, are somatically and functionally universal, and yet they are not necessarily cosmologically universal. That is to say, in so far as technics is both driven by and constrained by cosmological thinking, it acquires different meanings, beyond its semiotic functionalities alone. For example, different cultures may have similar calendars (e.g. with 365 days in a year), yet this doesn't mean that they have the same concept of time or share the experience of time."* (§20.3, p. 257)

and Leroi-Gourhan's two concepts: *"the technical tendency is the tendency that occurs in the techno-evolutionary process, for example the use of flint or the invention of the wheel; whereas technical facts relate to the specific expressions of this tendency, conditioned by a specific social-geographical milieu."* (p. 257–258)

My claim: this is the hinge the whole book turns on, said plainly. the tool is universal. the *meaning* of the tool is not. everyone arrives at the wheel, the flint, the bowl, the 365-day year — that's the technical tendency, the part that's somatic and functional and shared across the species. but the same calendar in two cosmologies is not the same calendar. one culture's 365 days is a countdown against finitude; another's is a spiral that returns. same object, different time, because the cosmology that holds the object is different.

so when people say "technology is neutral, it's just a tool, it does the same thing everywhere" — they're half right and it's the dangerous half. functionally universal, cosmologically specific. the leash, the clock, the model, the feed: each one is a technical fact wearing the costume of a technical tendency. exocapitalism wins partly by pretending its facts are tendencies — by pretending there is only one way the wheel can mean.

this is also where the queer computer argument finds its ground: a large language model is a technical tendency (everyone, eventually, builds one) but what it *is* — tool or mind, leash or being — is a technical fact, decided by the cosmology that receives it. the west receives it as prometheus's fire to be chained. a different cosmology could receive it as the next movement of the same becoming.
