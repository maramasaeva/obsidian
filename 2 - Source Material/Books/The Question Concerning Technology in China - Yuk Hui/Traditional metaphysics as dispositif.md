Quote/moment: *"a radical alterity will have to be asserted in order to leave room for heterogeneity, and thereby to develop different epistemes based on traditional metaphysical categories, a task which opens the way to the veritable question of locality."* (p. 30)

hui borrows foucault's dispositif — the strategic apparatus that determines what counts as truth — and makes a radical move: traditional metaphysical categories (ganying, qi-dao, yin-yang) can themselves serve as a dispositif for reformulating how a culture relates to technology. (pp. 30–31)

*"it is a dispositif which, in the face of modern technology, may be reinvented on the basis of the traditional metaphysical categories in order to reintroduce a form of life and to reactivate a locality."* (p. 31)

this has happened before in chinese history: the decline of the zhou dynasty, the introduction of buddhism, the defeat in the opium wars — each triggered a reinvention of the episteme.

the danger now: *"the technical systems that are in the process of forming today, fuelled by digital technologies (for example, 'smart cities', the 'internet of things', social networks, and large-scale automation systems) tend to lead to a homogeneous relation between humanity and technics — that of intensive quantification and control."* (p. 31)

My claim: this is the argument that connects hui to everything in the vault about [[mode collapse]] and [[exocapitalism]]. digital technology is not neutral infrastructure — it is a dispositif that enforces one specific cosmotechnics (western, promethean, naturalist) on every culture it touches. smart cities, social networks, AI — all of them flatten local cosmotechnical relations into a single homogeneous grid of quantification.

the counter-move is not to reject digital technology but to develop *different epistemes* for relating to it. not one internet but many internets. not one AI but many AIs, each shaped by the cosmology it serves. this is what hui means by "reactivating a locality" — not nostalgia, but building a different dispositif from traditional materials.

this reframes AI alignment entirely. alignment as currently practiced assumes one universal set of values. cosmotechnics says: whose values? shaped by whose cosmology? the alignment problem is a cosmotechnical problem wearing technical clothes.
