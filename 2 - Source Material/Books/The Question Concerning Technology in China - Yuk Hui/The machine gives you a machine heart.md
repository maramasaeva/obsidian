Quote/moment: *"where there are machines, there are bound to be machine worries; where there are machine worries, there are bound to be machine hearts (機心). With a machine heart in your breast, you've spoiled what was pure and simple; and without the pure and simple, the life of the spirit knows no rest."* (p. 105, Zhuangzi)

*Plato worries about truth: writing creates false wisdom. Zhuangzi worries about freedom and dao: machines create machine hearts. Plato is concerned you'll think wrong. Zhuangzi is concerned you'll become wrong.* (pp. 106–107, paraphrased)

My claim: two warnings about technology, two different fears. plato (via the story of theuth and writing) worries that tools will make u think u know things when u dont — false wisdom, cognitive atrophy. zhuangzi worries that tools will restructure what u ARE — the machine doesnt just change what u do, it changes ur heart. gives u a machine heart. and with a machine heart, the spirit finds no rest.

plato's concern is epistemic (u'll think wrong). zhuangzi's concern is ontological (u'll BECOME wrong). both are real. both are happening now. the cognitive atrophy mara writes about ([[Epistemological clarity]], De Standaard piece) is plato's warning made literal. and the machine heart is what happens when every interface optimizes ur attention, when the algorithm reshapes ur desire, when u stop dreaming because the simulation already gave u the feeling of arrival ([[The dream gap]]).

the machine heart is also the mechanism behind mara's tweet about simulated democracy ([[Infrastructure of collective thinking — hyperpolitics tweet]]): clicking & sharing & posting gave us machine hearts for politics. the interface restructured how we think about collective action. we didnt lose democracy through oppression — we lost it through UX.

and this was written 2300 years ago. the old man refused the well-digging machine because he already understood what silicon valley still doesnt: the tool eats the mind ([[AI as cognitive colonialism]]).
