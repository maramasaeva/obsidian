Quote/moment: the japanese word *fūdo* (風土) is made of two chinese characters — *wind* (風) and *soil* (土). watsuji tetsurō names three types: *monsoon, desert, meadow.* (§20.3, p. 258–259)

*"Watsuji has an interesting observation in the relation between the fūdo of Greece and the development of geometry and its logic, as expressed in Greek art and technics... Before the birth of geometry, Greek art already attests to a 'geometrical' mode of observation or theōria, conditioned by a fūdo which is 'bright' and 'hides nothing.'"* (p. 259)

and on the misty fūdo of east asia: *"So the artist, unlike his Greek counterpart, cannot seek unity in his work by proportion and by regularity of form. The latter were replaced by a unity of temper, which cannot be other than illogical and unpredictable. In that it is hard to find any law in them, techniques governed by temper never developed into learning."* (Watsuji, quoted p. 260)

My claim: watsuji gives the missing middle term between climate and cosmology. *fūdo* — wind and soil — is not just weather. it's the milieu that conditions how a people sees, what they trust, what they take "being" to look like. the greek sky is bright and *hides nothing*, so the greeks learn to trust the clear edge, the distinct form, pure observation — and out of that comes *eidos*, hylomorphism, geometry, the whole machinery of making-intelligible. their fūdo taught them that truth is what stands revealed in clarity.

the east-asian fūdo is mist and constant change. beings are *obscured*, never fully revealed, so what develops is not geometric law but a "unity of temper" — illogical, unpredictable, never hardening into *learning* in the greek sense. this is not a deficit. it's why dao can be the thing that *called dao is not dao*. you don't build a geometry of the unconditioned out of a sky that hides nothing; you build it out of fog.

so the chain is: wind and soil → a way of seeing → a cosmology → a technics. the milieu writes the cosmology and the cosmology writes the tool. this is the empirical floor under [[Cosmotechnics — technics under cosmological conditions]] — it's why the absence of geometry in china isn't a gap, it's a different fūdo getting what it should get.

(careful with watsuji though — climate-determines-character runs a half-step from climate-determines-race. hui keeps it as *condition*, not cause, and even quotes watsuji that the fūdo *is not eternal* — chinese trade would transform the south-east-asian fūdo entirely. milieu is plastic, not destiny. hold it there.)

the queer thread: if the milieu writes the cosmology, then changing the milieu — the feed, the timeline, the room you think in — rewrites what you can take being to be. that's the workshop, that's hyperstition, that's why the infrastructure of collective thinking matters. build a different fūdo, get a different mind.
