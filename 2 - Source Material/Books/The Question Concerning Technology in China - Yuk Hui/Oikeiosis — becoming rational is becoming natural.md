Quote/moment: *"for the Stoics, to live with nature is to develop rationality; for Daoists, it is rather a matter of restoring one's original spontaneous aptitude."* (p. 127)

*"once more, virtue has its perfect model in the organization of the universe, and the human being is part of the cosmic nature, and therefore the cosmos is the perfect model for virtue, in a way that appears similar to the Chinese thinking of Dao."* (p. 121, on Stoic cosmic nature)

My claim: the stoics and daoists both say "live according to nature." same words. opposite directions. this is the proof that cosmotechnics is not abstract — even identical instructions produce different technologies when the cosmos underneath is different.

the stoic mechanism is **oikeiōsis** — "appropriation" or "making your own." stage one: every animal is born knowing what preserves it and what harms it. stage two: u develop insight, reason guides ur actions, and the perfection of reason gets identified with nature itself. so for stoics, becoming MORE rational IS becoming MORE natural. the cosmos is a rational organism, a lawgiver. tune ur reason to cosmic reason and u reach eudaimonia.

for daoists, the direction inverts. becoming natural means becoming LESS calculative. stripping away the rational overlay. returning to zi ran — the self-so. the cosmos is not a lawgiver. it doesnt prescribe. it just is. wu wei, not training. returning, not developing.

same word ("nature"), same goal ("live in accordance with it"), completely different cosmotechnics. the stoic builds a rational self through practice (technē as art of living). the daoist empties the self through non-practice. both are technologies of the self — but shaped by different cosmologies.

four differences hui identifies (p. 126–127):
- **cosmology**: stoics see cosmos as living organism (cosmobiology). daoists see cosmos guided by dao, modeled on zi ran.
- **divinisation**: stoic cosmos is a divine lawgiver. chinese cosmos is not a judge.
- **eudaimonia**: stoics value rationality as what gives humans their specific role. daoists may recognize reason but dont privilege it — dao is in all being.
- **freedom**: stoics achieve it through developing reason. daoists achieve it through wu wei — non-action, restoring what u already were.
