Quote/moment: *"In Chinese cosmology, one finds a sense other than vision, hearing, and touch. It is called Ganying (感應), literally meaning 'feeling' and 'response', and is often understood as 'correlative thinking'. I prefer to call it resonance, following Joseph Needham."* (p. 27)

*"It yields a 'moral sentiment' and further, a 'moral obligation' (in social and political terms) which is not solely the product of subjective contemplation, but rather emerges from the resonance between the Heaven and the human, since the Heaven is the ground of the moral."* (p. 27)

the foundation: **天人合一 (tianren heyi)** — the unification of human and heaven. and the unification of **Qi (器, tools) and Dao (道, the way)**: the tool and the path are not separate.

fuxi created the eight trigrams by looking up at the sky and down at the earth — *"to show fully the attributes of the spirit-like and intelligent (operations working secretly), and to classify the qualities of the myriads of things."* (p. 28, from the Xi Ci)

My claim: ganying is the mechanism that makes chinese cosmotechnics work. it's not metaphor — it's the claim that the cosmos responds to human action and human feeling responds to the cosmos. you act, heaven responds. heaven moves, you feel it. the loop is real.

this is structurally identical to [[hyperstition]] but from the opposite direction. hyperstition says: fiction reaches forward and makes itself real. ganying says: the cosmos reaches inward and makes itself felt. both are feedback loops between human intention and reality. the difference is that hyperstition is promethean (you *make* the future happen) while ganying is receptive (you *resonate with* what is already unfolding).

the unification of Qi and Dao — tools and the way — is the chinese answer to the bifurcation simondon describes. in the west, technics and religion split apart. in china, the tool never fully separated from the path. chinese medicine is the clearest example: yin-yang, wu xing, harmony describe both the cosmos and the body. the technical act of healing is simultaneously a cosmological act.
