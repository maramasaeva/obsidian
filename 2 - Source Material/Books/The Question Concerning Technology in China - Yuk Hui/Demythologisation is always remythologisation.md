Quote/moment: *"Every demythologisation is accompanied by a remythologisation, since philosophy is conditioned by an origin from which it can never fully detach itself."* (p. 11)

and: *"technics thus understood, as an ontological category, I will argue, must be interrogated in relation to a larger configuration: a 'cosmology' proper to the culture from which it emerged."* (p. 10)

the romantics and german idealists already knew this — *"mythology must become philosophical, and the people rational, and philosophy must become mythological in order to make philosophers sensuous. Then eternal unity reigns among us."* (the oldest system-programme of german idealism, 1797, cited p. 11)

My claim: this is [[hyperstition]] seen from the other direction. hyperstition says: fiction makes itself real. hui says: every attempt to strip away myth produces new myth. the loop is the same loop. rationalism *is* a mythology — it's the myth that says there are no myths. and the harder it tries to purify itself of mythological residue, the more mythological it becomes. 

this matters for AI because the alignment community treats its own framework as demythologised — as pure rationality applied to existential risk. but the promethean structure (steal fire, gain knowledge, suffer consequences, save humanity through technical mastery) *is* the myth they're enacting. they just can't see it because they think they've outgrown myth.

the question is not whether we are telling myths. we are always telling myths. the question is whether we know which one we're in.
