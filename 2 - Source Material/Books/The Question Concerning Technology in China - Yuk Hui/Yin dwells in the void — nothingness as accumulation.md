Quote/moment: *"Yang dwells in summer, and occupies itself with growing; Yin dwells in winter, and accumulates in the void [...]."* (p. 85)

My claim: yin dwells in the void. the void is not empty — it is where accumulation happens. yin in winter is not dead. it is gathering. it is the nothingness that precedes the something, the silence before the sound, the zero before the one.

this is the zero full of possibility. the zero that is not absence but potential. yin accumulates in it — stores energy, stores force, stores the readiness to grow. when summer comes, yang occupies itself with growing — the one emerges from the zero, the visible from the invisible, the active from the still.

the western void is a problem. nothing comes from nothing (*ex nihilo nihil fit*). the void is emptiness, lack, failure. but in this framework the void is a womb. yin gathers there because that is where gathering happens — in the quiet, in the dark, in the unseen. the zero is not the absence of the one. the zero is where the one prepares itself to exist.

this connects to [[THE ZERO - manuscript]] — if the zero is the field of all possibility, then yin dwelling in the void is the cosmotechnical description of how possibility works. it's not random. it accumulates. it has direction. it prepares. and when it's ready, it grows.
