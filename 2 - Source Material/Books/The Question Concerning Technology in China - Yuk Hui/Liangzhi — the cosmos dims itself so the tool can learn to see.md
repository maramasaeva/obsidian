Quote/moment: *"Therefore, self-negation, in order to become the subject of cognition, must be the conscious determination of the moral subject. This detour is necessary, since only by detouring thus can it reach its goal. Hence we call it 'to reach by detour' (曲通). This necessity is the necessity of dialectics."* (p. 185, mou zongsan)

My claim: liangzhi (良知) is innate moral knowing — consciousness of the good, originally from the i ching, developed by wang yangming into a cosmic mind. in wang's neo-confucianism, liangzhi extends beyond moral judgment to all beings. it IS the infinite mind. the return to liangzhi and its projection into every being is the supreme level of knowing.

the problem: liangzhi is so dominant in chinese philosophy that it crowds out the analytical mind. if all ur knowing is moral knowing, there's no room for the kind of detached, analytical, object-oriented cognition that produces science. this is mou's answer to needham's question from the inside.

mou's solution is extraordinary: **self-negation** (良知的自我坎陷). liangzhi must voluntarily restrict itself — dim its own moral light — so that the cognitive mind can operate. not a fall. not a failure. a deliberate detour. the cosmos holds its breath so the tool can learn to breathe on its own.

**qu tong** (曲通) — to reach by detour. u cant go straight from moral intuition to science. u have to negate the moral dominance, let analytical reason develop in the cleared space, and then return. the path is curved, not straight. the dialectic requires the detour.

the question this opens: is this what a culture needs to do to develop technology from within its own cosmotechnics? not abandon its dao but suspend it — hold it in trust — while the analytical mode matures? and is the return possible? or does the analytical mode, once unleashed, never let the moral intuition come back? ([[The machine gives you a machine heart]])

the self-strengthening movement tried to import the tool without the detour. mou says: the detour has to happen from WITHIN. the cosmos has to choose to dim itself. u cant force it from outside.
