Quote/moment: the prometheus myth produced greek cosmotechnics — technology as transgression, truth-seeking, violence. but what produced the prometheus myth?

My claim: the stories come first. before the technology, before the philosophy, before the city — there is the story. the myth is not a reflection of a culture that already exists. the myth is the seed from which the culture grows. the greeks didn't write the prometheus story because they already had a violent relationship with nature. they *developed* that relationship because they told themselves a story about stolen fire, divine punishment, and the uncanny human who oversteps limits.

this is [[hyperstition]] at the civilisational level. the story reached forward in time and made itself real. the prometheus myth is a hyperstition that took thousands of years to fully actualise — and its final form is silicon valley, AI, the whole promethean apparatus of truth-extraction and limit-breaking.

so the question is: what made the greeks tell *that* story instead of a different one? what was the seed of the seed? was it geography — the aegean, the islands, the need to navigate and trade and compete? was it the specific separation between gods and humans that their cosmology assumed? was it something in the experience of making fire, of metallurgy, of the forge — the actual felt experience of transforming material through heat?

we don't know. maybe it doesn't matter. what matters is the implication: if the story comes first, and the story shapes the cosmology, and the cosmology shapes the technology — then **changing the story changes everything downstream**.

if we want to change what AI does and what it will do, we need to tell different stories. not better engineering. not better alignment. different *myths*. different origin stories for what technology is and what it's for. the stories are the beginning. they are part of the zero. they are part of the [[tao]] — the ground from which everything emerges.

stories are cosmotechnical primitives. they are the smallest unit of civilisational direction. change the story, change the cosmos, change the tool.

this is why making art matters. this is why [[The dream gap]] matters. this is why [[AGI's natural enemy is the dream]] matters. the dream is a story that hasn't been told yet. telling it is the most powerful technical act available.
