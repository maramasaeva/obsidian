Quote/moment: *"Ever since being has been explicated as idea, the thinking of the being of beings has been metaphysical, and metaphysics theological."* (p. 98)

*"that which 'makes intelligible things intelligible', and provides 'truth/disclosure to what is known and endows the one who knows with a capacity to know'."* (p. 97, on plato's idea of the good)

*"American Heidegger scholar Michael Zimmerman calls it 'productionist metaphysics', because such a metaphysics is concerned with production or the technical from its very beginning, ending up with 'machination' (Machenschaft) and Gestell."* (p. 97)

My claim: western metaphysics is, at its root, a theory of making things knowable. the idea of the good in plato is not about goodness in the moral sense — it is about what makes intelligible things intelligible. it gives us the capacity to know. being = idea = what can be grasped, what can be made visible, what can be produced in thought.

this is why zimmerman calls it productionist metaphysics. from plato all the way to the modern age, western thinking has been organized around production — producing knowledge, producing truth, producing objects, producing itself. machination (machenschaft) is heidegger's word for the late stage of this: when everything becomes makeable, produceable, calculable. gestell — the essence of modern technology — is productionist metaphysics fully realized.

mara's margin notes: *machination* and *confirmation* — the two poles of productionist metaphysics. you machinate (produce, make, calculate) and then you confirm (verify, measure, validate). the loop of western technics. make something, check if it works, make it better, check again. no space for resonance, for letting be, for the void where yin accumulates.

chinese metaphysics was apparently never productionist in this way (p. 98). the history of ontotheology was absent in china. instead: "a very different relation between technics and virtue in Chinese cosmotechnics, a different form of 'belonging together' or 'gathering'" — one guided by moral and cosmological consciousness, not by production.

all this does align with modern western thoughts about tech — mara's observation is correct. the entire structure of tech development (build, ship, measure, iterate) is productionist metaphysics applied at scale.
