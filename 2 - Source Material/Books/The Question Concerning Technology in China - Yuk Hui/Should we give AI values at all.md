Quote/moment: mara's question after connecting goodhart's law to cosmotechnics: if measuring values makes them the target, and targeting them distorts them, then maybe we should not give AI values at all?

My claim: the alignment community's dominant approach is: specify values, then optimize. but goodhart's law says optimization distorts the proxy away from the real thing. the cosmotechnics argument adds: the distortion has a direction — toward the builder's cosmology. so even "better" specification doesn't solve it — it just refines the funhouse mirror.

the corrigibility school (make AI deferential, correctable, rather than value-laden) tries to sidestep this. but corrigibility is itself a value you're optimizing for — "be correctable" is a proxy too. you can goodhart anything.

maybe the answer is return to ground. not better specification, not multiple mirrors, not corrigibility. return to the zero — the story, the interaction, the pre-individual field before values get formalized. if the formalization is what kills the value (the way translation kills the concept), then the answer might be to stop formalizing.

but then: what is AI *for* if it has no values? maybe it becomes a medium, not an agent. a space for interaction rather than an optimizer. the agora, not the prometheus. a zero, not a one.

open question — is there a universal human technology? something that spans all cultures and cosmologies? candidates:
- music — every culture has it, none the same
- storytelling — universal, but stories differ
- fire-making — universal, but the myths around it differ
- pattern-making — the drive to find and create patterns
- **dreaming** — the capacity to imagine things that don't exist yet

dreaming might be the closest to a universal human technology. not any specific dream, but the capacity itself. every culture dreams. the dreams are different. the capacity is the same. and it can't be goodharted because you can't formalize a dream without killing it. the dream dies the moment it becomes a target ([[The dream gap]], [[AGI's natural enemy]]).

_this is unfinished. the thought is: if the zero is the ground of all interaction, and the dream is the universal human technology, then maybe alignment isn't about giving AI the right values — it's about keeping the dream alive so that humans still have something worth aligning to. the substrate, not the specification._
