Quote/moment: *"the only hope for China to avoid the total destruction of its civilisation in the Anthropocene is to invent a new form of thinking and invention"* (p. 197)

*we must instead reinvent a cosmotechnics for our time.* (p. 201)

My claim: the whole book has been showing what was lost. now hui says what to do: not go back, not combine, not import — INVENT. a new cosmotechnics that uses the worldwide network we already have but doesn't surrender to the single destiny that network currently carries.

this is not synthesis. u dont take chinese cosmotechnics and western cosmotechnics and blend them. that was the self-strengthening movement's mistake. and it's not nostalgia. u dont revive the qin and the se and pretend modernity didn't happen. it's invention. creating something that didn't exist before, grounded in cosmological consciousness but adequate to the technical reality of now.

the key move: find **another interface between what mou called noumenal and phenomenal ontology** (p. 197). not mou's idealist version (self-negation of liangzhi). not the marxist version (dialectics of nature). something new. developing qi without detaching it from dao and cosmological consciousness.

this is the project. everything before was diagnosis. this is the prescription.
