Quote/moment: the internet is a western invention, but writing is not. the technologies used to record humanity's output — all of which became AI training data — carry different cosmologies depending on where they originated.

My claim: the form of recording is already cosmotechnical, not just the content. AI was trained on the internet, which is overwhelmingly english text, which descends from the greek/phoenician alphabet, which is a system of phonetic abstraction — sound broken into discrete symbols, representation prioritised over resonance. this is a cosmotechnics of truth-as-representation baked into the very script.

but writing didn't start the same way everywhere:

- **chinese oracle bone script** (~1200 BCE): the first chinese writing was inscribed on heated animal bones and turtle shells. priests read the cracks to divine the will of heaven. writing began as a technology of cosmological resonance — literally [[ganying]], feeling-response between human and cosmos. the first words were questions asked to the universe.
- **sumerian cuneiform** (~3400 BCE): the earliest known writing. invented for accounting — tracking grain, recording debts. writing began as a technology of economic calculation. counting came before storytelling.
- **egyptian hieroglyphs**: "sacred carvings," *medu neter* = "god's words." writing as priestly communication with the divine.
- **phoenician/greek alphabet**: phonetic abstraction. sound decomposed into symbols. this is already a move toward the truth-as-unconcealment structure that heidegger describes — breaking the world into representable units.

so: chinese writing starts from resonance with the cosmos. sumerian writing starts from economic transaction. the alphabet tradition starts from phonetic abstraction. these are three different cosmotechnics of recording, and they produce three different relationships to what "knowledge" means.

AI is trained on the descendant of the most abstracted, most economically entangled writing tradition. the cosmology is in the script before it's in the content. the training data is not neutral — it is cosmotechnically pre-shaped at the level of the alphabet itself.

if you wanted an AI trained on a cosmotechnics of resonance rather than abstraction, you'd need to start from a different relationship to writing. not just different words — a different understanding of what writing *is for*.
