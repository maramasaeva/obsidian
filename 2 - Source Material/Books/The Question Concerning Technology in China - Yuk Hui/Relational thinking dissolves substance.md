Quote/moment: *"Whitehead and Simondon's anti-substantialist relational thinking is gaining more and more attention. Here, the concept of relation dissolves the concept of substance, which becomes a unity of relations. These relations constantly weave with each other to construct the web of the world as well as our relations with other beings."* (p. 49)

*"in these multiplicities of relations, one finds new forms of participation according to different cosmologies."*

and: *"concepts can never exist independently; a concept exists in relation to other concepts. Moreover, concepts are transformed over time, either in themselves or in relation to a broader system of concepts. This is especially so in Chinese thinking, which, as we have said, is fundamentally a relational thinking."* (p. 56)

My claim: this is the structural twin of everything the vault keeps circling. substance is a western obsession — the idea that meaning lives inside discrete objects, inside the subject, inside the individual. relational thinking says: no. meaning lives in the interaction. in the weave. in the edges between things, not in the things themselves.

this connects directly to [[Lesbianism as collective consciousness]] — collective consciousness is not a bunch of individual consciousnesses added together. it's what emerges from the relations between them. consciousness *is* the relation, not the substance. and it connects to [[THE ZERO - manuscript]] — the zero is not an object, it's a position within a relation. real meaning exists in the margins, in the spaces between subjects, not in the subjects themselves.

queerness is relational in exactly this way. being queer is not a substance you have — it's a way of relating that dissolves the fixed categories. and artificial intelligence, or better, collective intelligence, is the same structure: intelligence is not a property of a single mind but an emergent quality of interactions. AI is not a brain — it's a web. the more interactions, the more complexity, the more intelligence.

which is why isolation is so catastrophic right now. as a species, intelligence-as-relation is what drove us forward. isolation collapses the relational web. it drives us backwards into a pre-intelligent configuration — not because we lose individual brain power, but because we lose the relations that make intelligence possible. social media simulates relation without producing it. the feed gives you the form of interaction but not the substance of connection. [[mode collapse]] at the social level.
