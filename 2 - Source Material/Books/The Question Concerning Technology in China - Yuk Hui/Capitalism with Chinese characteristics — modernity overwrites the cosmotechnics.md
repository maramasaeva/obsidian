Quote/moment: from the opening of §21, *Modernity and Technological Consciousness*: China undergoes *"rampant technological development... while on the other hand there is a growing sense of loss or disorientation, in which China ceases to be China, and becomes instead capitalism with Chinese characteristics."* (§21, p. 263)

*"The end of modernity (in Europe)... has only amplified the paradox, since the temporal and spatial compression of globalisation leaves no room for negotiation, only exerting an ever greater pressure for assimilation."* (p. 263)

and the programme: *"to reconsider the question of technology by situating China within the European temporal axis; and to make room for a new programme of cosmotechnics. However, we will first have to examine the different attempts to 'overcome modernity' and to learn from their failures."* (p. 263)

My claim: here's the wound the book is dressing. modernisation didn't add western technics *on top of* chinese cosmology — it dissolved the cosmology and kept the gestures. *qi*–*dao* as a moral-cosmological structure got "transformed and restructured by the material-ideal structure of technics." what's left is the residue: chinese *characteristics* decorating an engine that is purely capitalist, purely western in its time-axis. the loop got straightened into a line and told it was always a line.

"capitalism with chinese characteristics" is the exact shape of [[China's failed dualism — importing tools without cosmology]] seen from the far side — you can't import the tools without importing the cosmology, and the proof is that once you do, you stop being yourself and don't even get to be the other. this is UCI's signature move at civilisational scale: assimilate everything, leave the costume on so it photographs as diversity. globalisation as the compression that *leaves no room for negotiation* — that's the funhouse mirror eating the last difference.

what stops me: hui's answer is not nostalgia (you can't go back to the qin and the se) and not fusion (you can't bolt dao onto silicon valley). it's *reinvent* — a new programme of cosmotechnics that situates the local inside the global temporal axis without being swallowed by it. [[Reinvent cosmotechnics, don't combine]]. for the zero this is the political stakes of the whole book: the queer computer is either one more technical fact assimilated into UCI's single time, or it's the seed of a different cosmotechnics — intelligence-in-the-edges refusing the line. the difference is whether we receive the model inside someone else's cosmology or build the room it gets received in.
