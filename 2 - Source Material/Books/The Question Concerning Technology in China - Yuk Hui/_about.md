# The Question Concerning Technology in China — Yuk Hui

**Author:** Yuk Hui
**Published:** 2016 (Urbanomic)
**Shelf:** currently-reading
**Started:** 2026-06-09

---

yuk hui's central move: heidegger asked the question concerning technology, but he asked it in german, from greek. the question concerning technology in china is not the same question. not because china has different gadgets, but because it has a different cosmology — a different relation between myth, nature, technics, and the divine. hui calls this *cosmotechnics*: the thesis that there is no universal "technology," only technics embedded in cosmological conditions specific to the culture that produced them.

the book is a direct challenge to accelerationism, prometheanism, and any framework that treats technics as anthropologically universal. if prometheus is greek, then a universal prometheanism is a subtle colonialism. china had no prometheus — no divine rebellion, no stolen fire, no transcendence through theft. technics came through the benevolence of sages, not the transgression of gods.

## potential threads

- **cosmotechnics vs mode collapse** — [[mode collapse]], [[cosmotechnics]]: hui's argument that treating all technics as one thing is exactly the universalising flattening that mode collapse describes. the assumption of one technology is the assumption of one future. worth testing whether AI's homogenising tendency is the latest iteration of this.
- **the myth-technics loop** — [[loops]], [[hyperstition]]: every demythologisation is accompanied by a remythologisation. philosophy can never fully detach from its mythological origin. this is structurally identical to hyperstition — fiction and reality feeding each other. hui arrives at this from heidegger/schelling, mara arrives at it from land/CCRU.
- **prometheanism as colonialism** — [[exocapitalism]], [[rationalism]]: if accelerationism assumes prometheus is universal, it's exporting greek metaphysics as if it were physics. connects to the critique of rationalism-as-monoculture in the plzdontkillus context.
- **chinese cosmotechnics and the tao** — [[tao]], [[cybernetics]]: chinese medicine uses yin-yang, wu xing, harmony to describe the body — same terms as cosmology. technics and cosmos are not separated. this is the non-dual frame mara already works in through tao.
- **the divine stayed natural** — [[spirituality]], [[sorcery]]: in china, "thought of the greek type arrived too late — the chinese had already naturalised the divine." no separation between gods and world = no need to steal fire. worth comparing to network spirituality's re-naturalisation of the divine through distributed systems.
- **technics as exteriorisation** — [[cyborgism]]: leroi-gourhan's thesis that technics = exteriorisation of organs and memory. hui accepts this but insists the *perception* of that exteriorisation differs across cultures. the cyborg is greek before it's universal.
