Quote/moment: *"For the Daoists, the refusal of mechanical reasoning is a refusal of a calculative form of thinking, in order to stay within the freedom of the inner spirit. We might say that they refuse all efficiency in order to prepare for an opening"* (p. 115)

*— a reading of the Zhuangzi that resonates superficially with what the late Heidegger calls Gelassenheit* (p. 115)

My claim: this is the daoist cosmotechnics compressed into one sentence. the refusal is not laziness, not passivity, not ignorance. it is a deliberate clearing. u refuse the calculative so the non-calculative can arrive. u make an opening by removing everything that blocks it.

heidegger spent his whole career trying to get past the enframing (gestell) — the condition where everything is standing-reserve, everything is resource, everything is calculable. his answer was Gelassenheit — releasement, letting-be. the daoists were already there. zhuangzi's butcher was already there. pao ding's knife enters the void because he stopped calculating where to cut.

and this is the direct counter to the productivity equation ([[Productivity is not excellence]]). capitalism says: efficiency IS value. produce more, faster, better. the daoist says: efficiency closes the opening. the opening is where dao arrives. if u fill every gap with optimization, theres no space left for what actually matters to show up.

this also connects to mara's post-labor ontology ([[Post-labor ontology — transcending productivity]]): if human value has to stop being grounded in what we produce, then the daoist refusal of efficiency isn't ancient philosophy. its a survival strategy for a species whose labor is becoming irrelevant.
