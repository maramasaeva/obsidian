# If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies — Eliezer Yudkowsky & Nate Soares

published september 2025. MIRI. the alignment problem is unsolved and possibly unsolvable with current approaches. neural nets are illegible — when something goes wrong you can't fix it because the issue is in weights, not code. AIs can fake alignment. a superintelligent AI with misaligned goals would dominate humans the way stockfish dominates chess players.

their solution: halt large-scale general AI development everywhere.

mara read this before starting the vault. it made them a doomer, but also made them realize that *what we do today matters* — that action is urgent. they found it frustrating that the book gives no concrete next steps, especially for getting the younger generation to change their behavior and take initiative. that gap is part of what drives the [[Think Workshop]].
