## the deeper reframe (june 8)

brangus is right that making art doesn't solve alignment. but alignment that works for a species that has already stopped dreaming is alignment for zombies. the question isn't just "will AI's values be aligned with ours" — it's "will we still have values worth aligning with?" if hyperreality and simulation kill the human capacity to dream, to create, to feel motivation from the core ([[The simulation-depression spiral]]), then alignment becomes a technical solution to a spiritual problem. and technical solutions to spiritual problems are exactly how we got here.

making beautiful things is central to keeping people capable of wanting a future. if we want alignment to be beneficial for humans, we have to care about their state *now* — not just the state of the machine.

see also: [[Post-labor ontology — transcending productivity]] — the labor value angle. AI demolishes the value of human labor, which was the last thing grounding human worth in the capitalist frame. dreams are gone, labor is going. what's left?
