## the core thread

the stories we tell become the reality we live in. this isn't metaphor — it's mechanism. [[hyperstition]]: fictions that make themselves real. ada palmer showed it with the renaissance (historians fabricated golden ages that *became* history). the hero narrative isn't neutral — it's a political technology that centers conflict, domination, a single protagonist imposing order. it's also the shape of [[UCI]]: one agent, one goal, maximum force.

but there's another shape. ursula le guin's carrier bag: the first human tool wasn't a weapon but a container. stories don't have to be spears. they can be bags — holding many things in relation, without collapsing them into one arc. this is [[tao]]. this is [[loops]]. this is what [[queer]] storytelling already does.

**the alignment connection:** if telling the right stories is a way to shift reality, then the stories we tell about AI — about alignment, about risk, about what intelligence is — are themselves a technology. alignment-as-hero-story produces the paperclip maximizer (one agent, one goal). alignment-as-carrier-bag means holding many values in relation without resolving them. the shape of the story determines the shape of the future.

**the practical question:** can we actively use this? can we do research into how stories have had certain consequences, and then use that knowledge to tell stories that decrease x-risk? not as propaganda — you have to actually believe it. it's a weird epistemic place: knowing something is true *because* you know it's true. but the placebo effect works. manifestation works. hyperstition works. what if we scale it?

## what i want to talk about at pdku

### 1. hyperstition as alignment technology
the stories we tell about AI become the AI we build. this is not new (CCRU, land, neolumerianism) but it IS new in the alignment community. nobody in the rationalist scene is making this connection explicitly. i need to get optimists together and tell a story — not with the *intention* of making it real (that might kill it), but with the actual *belief* that alignment is possible. giving up on alignment is itself a loss. see: [[The carrier bag as counter-hyperstition]], [[Alignment as gathering not domination]].

### 2. grimes, moloch, and the dance
she said beauty is on the side of the biological — the dancing god vs the cold god. then she said slop makes artists better — which means moloch is directing the dance. these can't both be true. i've already made a video about the first tweet ([[Music is cosmotechnics — grimes, ganying, and the last musician]]) and posted a tweet responding to the slop argument ([[Grimes slop tweet — mara's reply]]). this is a conversation i want to have with her in person at pdku.

### 3. the hero story as x-risk amplifier
ada palmer's point via anna howard / wild geese: the hero narrative is empire propaganda. it legitimizes domination. and it's the default shape of how we talk about AI futures — the singularity, the one agent that takes over, the superintelligence that either saves or destroys. what if that narrative shape is *part of the problem*? what if telling AI stories as hero stories is what makes AI dangerous? see: [[The carrier bag as counter-hyperstition]].

### 4. stories kids tell online
younger generations are posting stories — memes, threads, manifestos, worldbuilding — that function as hyperstition whether they know it or not. the stories they put online shape the future. we can actively use this knowledge: teach kids that the stories they tell matter, that they're not just "posting" — they're casting spells. this is media literacy meets sorcery.

### 5. kotopia as positive hyperstition / milady as dark kawaii
certain art movements are already doing this in online spheres. [[kotopia]] (koto's project) is doing it in a positive way — building a world, a community, a future through art and music and collective imagination. [[milady]] is doing something different — telling a story of love through brutalist kawaii aesthetics, touching people through the contradiction. *"there is no meme. i love you."* — a meme that denies being a meme while delivering love. see: [[There is no meme, I love you]]. both are hyperstition engines. the question is what future they're building.

### 6. music as the technology
music is cosmotechnics — the technology through which the connection between cosmos and creature becomes audible (yuk hui, ganying, the qin and the se). grimes described this without naming it. if AI composes music, whose cosmos is it resonating with? the instrument carries the cosmology of whoever built it. this connects to everything: the tao, alignment, the question of whether AI can channel or only simulate. and right now, reading laozi's dao de jing (ken liu translation, p. 78 of 163), the tao connects all of these threads — music, stories, the future, the way. see: [[Music is cosmotechnics — grimes, ganying, and the last musician]].

### 7. the tao as connective tissue
the tao is not a separate topic — it's the thread that runs through everything else. the carrier bag is tao (gathering, not forcing). wu wei is the counter to rationalist agency (jullien's *treatise on efficacy*). music as cosmotechnics is tao made audible. alignment-as-gathering is tao applied to AI. the stories we tell about the future are tao if they follow the way, and anti-tao if they force a single path. laozi's dao de jing is the text that holds all of this together.
