## premise

the tao is not a separate topic — it's the thread running through every other video idea. this could be the connective video, the one that ties everything together. or it could be a series of moments woven through the other videos rather than its own standalone piece.

## the threads

- **carrier bag = tao.** le guin's story-as-container, not weapon. gathering, holding, returning. this is the shape of the tao — it doesn't force, it holds. wu wei as narrative principle
- **wu wei vs rationalist agency.** jullien's *treatise on efficacy*: the west forms a model and forces reality to match. the tao reads the propensity of the situation and lets the result come of itself. the entire EA/rationalist worldview is the maximalist western mode. what if alignment needs the other mode?
- **music as tao made audible.** ganying — the resonance between cosmos and creature, mediated by sound. the tao doesn't argue. it resonates
- **the spiral = the loop = the torus.** growth is not linear ([[loops]], [[Spyrodynamism]]). stories are not arrows. the tao returns
- **queer as tao.** queer existence is perpetual becoming — not fixed identity but continuous process. "neither resolution nor stasis, but continuing process" (le guin). that's tao
- **laozi's dao de jing** (ken liu translation) — currently reading, p. 78 of 163. the text that holds all of this together. specific passages TBD
