there's this book called machine decision is not final, it's a recent urbanomic release, and there's a line in it that says if we want a perfectly accurate system we must turn people into error-free components too. and that the only map with perfect accuracy is a map drawn on a one-to-one scale with reality. and i keep thinking about that because i think what it describes is exactly what's happening to art right now. we've been confusing three pairs of words that are related but not the same thing. art and content. creation and production. inspiration and consumption. and those are not synonyms. they've been *made* to feel like synonyms because the system needs them to be interchangeable.

production is making something so that something else can be made from it. production for the sake of other production only. u produce content so that content produces engagement so that engagement produces revenue. it's margins all the way down. the thing u made is never the point — the margin it generates is the point.

and creation is the opposite. creation is making something purely for the act of making it. not for what it produces next. the process is the goal, the loop is the goal, not the output.
