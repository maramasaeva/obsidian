## premise

music is not entertainment. music is the technology through which the connection between cosmos and creature becomes audible. if the first video ([[Music is cosmotechnics — grimes, ganying, and the last musician]]) asked "who will be the last musician?", this one asks: what if music is how we solve alignment?

## the argument

- the instrument carries the cosmology of whoever built it. the qin carried confucian cosmology. the organ carried christian cosmology. AI music carries the cosmology of optimization, engagement, efficiency
- but: if we build instruments (AI or otherwise) that carry a *different* cosmology — one of resonance, of gathering, of tao — then the music that comes through them shapes the future differently
- laozi's dao de jing (ken liu translation, reading now — p. 78 of 163) connects this: the tao is the connective tissue. music-as-tao means music-as-alignment. not alignment in the rationalist sense (one agent, one goal) but alignment in the taoist sense (resonance, harmony, wu wei)
- the importance of music specifically: it moves through bodies, it bypasses the rational filter, it creates collective experience. if hyperstition works through stories, music is the most powerful delivery mechanism
- messier (mara's own music) is an example: making music that carries a cosmology, not just a vibe
