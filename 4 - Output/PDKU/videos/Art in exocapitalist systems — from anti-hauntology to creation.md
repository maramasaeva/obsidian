## connection

the previous video ([[Anti-hauntology and the primal impulse — script]]) ends on the claim that the image industry has destroyed the horizons of hope by collapsing the gap between desire and fulfillment. if reality is fiction and fiction is reality, then desire gets satisfied at the level of the image before it ever becomes a drive toward anything. hauntology = this despair. anti-hauntology = the logic of hope that might come after.

but that video doesn't answer *how* the collapse happens mechanically. the note on [[Art in Exocapitalist systems]] does.

the exocapitalist system replaces three things:
- art → content (created to inspire → produced to be consumed)
- creation → production (for the act itself → for the margin it generates)
- inspiration → consumption (active → passive)

this is the machinery of the hope-collapse. when the system demands error-free perfection on all sides — perfect producers, perfect consumers, perfect content — it drives toward a virtual reality, a 1:1 map of the world. and a 1:1 map leaves no gap. no gap = no desire = no hope. the exocapitalist production loop is the *engine* of hauntology.

but the note also carries the exit: creation purely for the act of creation itself. the artist as medium, not producer. art as something that was already Real, waiting to be Actualised. this is the anti-hauntological move — not producing new content but actualising something that existed before the system captured it. spyrodynamism: the spiral that doesn't optimise, it unfolds.

## the argument for the video

1. pick up where the last video left off: the blind spots at plzdontkillus, the despair engine
2. name the mechanism: exocapitalism's three substitutions (art→content, creation→production, inspiration→consumption)
3. the perfection trap: a system that demands error-free components on all sides converges on virtual reality — and virtual reality is hauntology materialised
4. the exit: creation without purpose, the artist as medium. this connects back to linggan ([[Creativity as a neolemurian practice]]) — the breath, the fuzziness, the thing AI doesn't have yet
5. end on: if anti-hauntology is the logic of hope, then art (not content) is the practice of hope. making something purely to make it is the most radical act available.
