## the approach — sound as weapon

see [[PDKU sound design template]] for the full technical framework.

the thesis: everyone optimizes visuals. you optimize *sound*. the first 1.5 seconds of every video is an engram delivery system — a somatic pattern that writes itself into the viewer's nervous system before the content starts.

five layers: body (sub-bass), voice (the only conscious element), glitch (the knife), harmonic (the undertow), void (the loudest silence).

the arc repeats daily: hook → voice → build → turn → cut. after a few days, the viewer's body recognizes the shape. the shape IS the brand. not a logo — a somatic pattern the nervous system learns to crave.

sources: steve goodman / sonic warfare (bass materialism, engrams, audio virology), ASMR/frisson research (the paradox of simultaneous relaxation and arousal), infrasound research (subliminal cortisol spiking).
