## Script

so grimes tweeted something that i can't stop thinking about. she said — i'll read it — "i feel like on the other side of the moloch / autonomous capital demon there is an emergent divine beauty that wants to fill the universe with mozart. like in the back of my head i sort of imagine the cold god jousts with the dancing god, the biological substrate, and a perfect symmetry emerges."

and i love this. i do. but the thing is, the research she was quoting wasn't specifically about mozart. they just used mozart because its calming for most people. it applies to any music that moves you. which means the emergent divine beauty isn't the music. its the musician. its the one who channels it. the one through whom something cosmic becomes sound.

i've been reading this book — yuk hui, "the question concerning technology in china" — and he describes this concept called ganying. gan-ying. its from confucian philosophy and it means feeling-response. resonance. the idea that the human and the cosmos respond to each other. you act, heaven responds. heaven moves, you feel it.

and the proof they use — the literal proof for this being real — is two musical instruments. the qin and the se. two stringed instruments that harmonize when played together. and the confucians say this resonance between heaven and human is, quote, "as objective and concrete as the resonance of these musical instruments." its not a metaphor. the sound IS the evidence. you can hear the cosmos speaking through the instrument.

so music, in chinese cosmotechnics, is not entertainment. music is the technology through which the connection between heaven and human becomes audible. the instrument is the tool, the harmony is the way, and they're unified in the act of playing. music is cosmotechnics. the oldest kind.

and grimes is describing exactly this. without the word. the dancing god, the biological substrate, the perfect symmetry — thats ganying. the cosmos and the creature resonating through sound.

but then — ezekiel 28:13 says lucifer was the first musician. the most beautiful angel. the one who channeled divine resonance into sound before anyone else. and also the first one who fell. so even in the biblical tradition, the first musician is the first rebel. the one who brings the fire. the one who gets punished.

so who will be the last musician? if AI composes music, if AI performs, if AI resonates — is it a musician or a machine? does it channel something or does it simulate channeling? because the instrument carries the cosmology of whoever built it. the qin carried confucian cosmology. the organ carried christian cosmology. and AI music carries the cosmology of the people who trained it — optimization, engagement, efficiency.

grimes sees the emergent divine beauty on the other side of moloch. i see it too. but i wonder whose cosmos that beauty is resonating with. and whether we'll recognize the harmony. or just hear a perfect simulation of it.
