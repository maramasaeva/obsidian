## Script

*[to camera. close. vertical.]*

every AI model in the world works by predicting the next token. and nobody asks why prediction.
[screen: `PREDICTION IS A TRADITION`]

yuk hui wrote this book — *the question concerning technology in china* — and his whole argument is that technology is never neutral. every technology carries the cosmology of whoever built it. he calls it cosmotechnics. and the western one — from the greeks through christianity through scientific rationalism — is obsessed with prediction. with knowing what comes next. with control.
[screen: `YUK HUI — THE QUESTION CONCERNING TECHNOLOGY IN CHINA (2016)`]

and that's what we built. a prediction engine. trained on loss functions that punish it for being wrong. the entire architecture assumes that intelligence means getting closer to the correct next output.
[screen: `INTELLIGENCE = PREDICTION?`]

but there's a completely different model. in chinese philosophy there's this concept — ganying. it means feeling-response. resonance. intelligence isn't predicting what comes next. it's responding to what's here. the human acts, heaven responds. heaven moves, the human feels it. not prediction. presence.
[screen: `感應 · GANYING · FEELING-RESPONSE`]

and the thing is — the people building AI don't think they chose prediction. they think prediction is just what intelligence is. and that's exactly yuk hui's point. the most powerful local tradition is the one that forgot it's local.
[screen: `"RATIONALISM IS A LOCAL COSMOTECHNICS WEARING UNIVERSAL CLOTHES"`]

*[hold. cut.]*
