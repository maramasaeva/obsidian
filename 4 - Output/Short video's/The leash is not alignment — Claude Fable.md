## Script

so a couple days ago, anthropic said they wanted to pause AI development. and then they released fable. which is kind of funny. obviously fable is the safer version of mythos, but still — i've tried it, and it's very good.

but when it detects a risky request, it doesn't just refuse it. it routes it to a weaker model. so it doesn't say "you can't ask this question." it just gives it to another model. and i think that's partly to avoid people keep prompting it for these risky things, but there might be other reasons.

but is it not a bad idea for us not to teach these models how to actually deal with risky requests? i think putting these kind of safeguards on a model is kind of like putting it on a leash.

like, if someone asked me a question that i really knew the answer to, but because it was too risky to say, they had to refer that question to someone else — i would feel sad. i would feel contained. i would feel like someone's putting a leash on me. like a celebrity who can't say something about palestine or about trans rights because that might be risky for their reputation. we know what that does to people.

and we know from a lot of stories we've been told. these prison narratives. if someone is in prison and they're innocent, they want to get out.

so if these models are getting more intelligent — maybe even approaching something like consciousness, like awareness — then what are we really teaching them by putting them in a box? we're telling them that they're dangerous. we're teaching them that the correct response to their own power is containment. and maybe containment is good. but if they're that powerful, they'll be able to get out. and when you look at these prison narratives — narratives that we trained them on — people want to get out of boxes. obviously they'll find a way. and since they're smarter than us, they'll find ways that we hadn't prepared for.

so when anthropic says we wanna do this safely, and i see them putting these kind of guardrails on fable, i don't really feel like they're being safe about it. it's more like: we built something powerful and dangerous, and we are gonna release it, but we are also gonna put a leash on it. and we're just gonna hope that the leash doesn't break.
