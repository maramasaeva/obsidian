# pinterest's offline strategy — relevant for the workshop

pinterest is running a brand campaign encouraging users to *leave the platform and go offline*. their positioning: find inspiration online, go live it in real life.

- anthem film: 54M+ youtube views
- coachella activation: phone-free zone using Yondr pouches (phone lockers)
- pinterest CEO publicly supports social media bans for children under 16
- result: 18% revenue growth, 631M monthly active users, revenue surpassed $1 billion

the counterintuitive move — telling users to leave — actually strengthens loyalty and credibility. they compete on *intent* rather than *time spent*. AI delivers faster, more relevant recommendations so users spend less time but with higher purpose.

sara pollack (VP consumer marketing): *"finding inspiration online so that they can go and live offline."*

## why this matters for the workshop

- proof that even platforms are recognizing the problem
- the Yondr pouch idea could work in schools (phone-free workshop time)
- pinterest's CEO supporting under-16 bans = corporate ally for the policy argument
- shows kids that "go offline" isn't anti-tech — even tech companies are saying it
- the framing "find inspiration, then go live it" aligns perfectly with the workshop's message
