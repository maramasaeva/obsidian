# think workshop

workshop / presentation for schools in leuven. target: 16+ year olds. the premise is simple — take your time back, keep thinking, stop handing your cognition to machines and platforms before you've built it yourself.

live page: https://messier-systems.vercel.app/think

---

## the opening move

start with: *who are your role models? who do you look up to, who inspires you?*

kids name their favorite celebs, artists, musicians, whoever. then: *what do they all have in common?* they all have passion for something. they work to make something great. they take the TIME. that's the entry point — time, effort, and the willingness to actually think through something, is the thing that separates people who make things from people who scroll past them.

## opal

refer to [Opal](https://www.opal.so/) — an app for taking back your time. practical, not preachy. something they can actually use after the workshop.

## funding

approach a government/mutual institution — something like Christelijke Mutualiteit — to fund it. goal: kids don't have to pay for opal themselves. also good for opal as a startup, mutual benefit.
